Who Goes Where

TPB · Academy · Nurture

Every conversation ends in one of three places. Route on fit, never on what they can afford.

→ The Profitable Breeder
  • Needs clarity, education, fundamentals or tools
  • Not yet right for high-ticket implementation
  • Send the TPB VSL
→ Academy call
  • Established or current breeder
  • A meaningful problem, wants implementation
  • Urgency, readiness, and appropriate resources
  • Book the Academy call
→ Nurture
  • Not ready for either yet
  • Keep in the appropriate marketing / nurture path

Your DM Playbook

same stages every time

1 · Engage

Someone comments. Acknowledge publicly when it fits. The automation sends the first DM; you take over once they actually engage.

2 · Start a real conversation

Not "Want to book a call?" Instead: "Curious — are you currently breeding, or still preparing for your first litter?" Now you have context.

3 · Understand

How established are they? What are they struggling with? What have they tried? What do they want to change, and why now? Education or implementation?

4 · Route

TPB, Academy, or Nurture — using "Who Goes Where" above.

5 · Book

For Academy: book, confirm the time, and record qualification notes, source, primary pain, desired outcome and context so Austin walks on informed.

6 · Follow up

No response is not a dead lead. You own follow-up until they book, buy TPB, clearly decline, are disqualified, or reach the nurture point.

What You Do NOT Do

You are not the Social Media Manager. Yasmin owns content and distribution. You own the sales conversations social media creates — not content calendars, filming, editing, analytics, or what gets posted.
  • Give veterinary advice
  • Make scientific claims
  • Promise outcomes
  • Negotiate Academy pricing
  • Invent payment plans
  • Offer discounts
  • Change guarantees
  • Promise certification
  • Handle Member Success issues
  • Recommend Academy just because they can afford it
Until you're certified internally as a closer, don't try to close Academy in the DMs. Your job is to create enough desire and clarity to get the right person onto the call.
