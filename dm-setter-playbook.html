Your DM Setter Playbook

Everything you need to do this job well: what you own, how to qualify and route people, your daily targets, how the math works, exactly how you get paid, and how we will coach you to win. Read it once end to end, then keep it open while you work.

Your mission in one line: turn qualified Instagram conversations into TPB members and into booked, qualified sales calls for Academy / B.O.S.S. You run the front of the process. You make the right recommendation for each person, not the most expensive one. Do that consistently and you earn well.
01

What You Own

You own

  • Starting qualified new conversations from approved sources
  • Answering warm inbound (comments, story replies, DMs) fast
  • Real two-way conversations and qualification
  • Closing TPB directly ($97/mo) right in the DM
  • Booking + confirming qualified Academy / B.O.S.S. calls
  • Following up until a yes, a no, or a clear disqualify
  • Keeping your CRM accurate: stage, source, next action, outcome

You do not own

  • Closing the Academy / B.O.S.S. call (a closer does that, unless you're later trained and assigned)
  • Creating ads or content (Marketing)
  • Onboarding, support, or retention of members (Customer Success)
  • Running the community day to day (Community Manager)
  • Pricing or refund decisions (leadership)
The golden rule: qualify for fit first, product second. TPB is closed in the DM. Academy / B.O.S.S. is never priced or pitched in the DM beyond a single framing line, it is booked to a call. Your job is to get the right people to the right next step.

03

Routing: Where Each Person Goes

Offer TPB when…

  • They have a real problem TPB solves
  • $97/mo is realistic for them
  • They want structure and community now
  • They're not ready for (or not a fit for) a $3K+ build

Book a call when…

  • They're an operating breeder
  • Revenue or clear ambition is there
  • Budget + decision-maker + timeline
  • They want done-with-you / done-for-you help

Nurture when…

  • Good fit, wrong timing
  • Tag them, set a dated follow-up
  • This is not a disqualify, it's a "later"

Disqualify when…

  • No breeding intent, or can't pay
  • Competitor, or just browsing
  • Disrespectful
  • Tag + close it out, protect the calendar
Never put someone on a call just to hit a booking number. A call that was never qualified wastes the closer's time, hurts your show rate, and does not pay you (you're paid on attended, qualified calls, not bookings). Quality over volume, every time.

The rules, so it's always clear

  • A deal is yours when you're the first-touch owner in the CRM and you booked the call. Both logged.
  • Earned when the cash clears. Paid the next pay cycle after that. No pay for a verbal yes or for just booking a call.
  • Commissions are on cash actually collected, not contract value. Payment plans pay as the money comes in.
  • If a deal is refunded or charged back inside the guarantee window, that commission comes back out of your next payout. After the window, it's yours to keep.
  • If you're ever trained and assigned to close a deal yourself, you earn the closer payout on it, not both. One or the other, whichever is higher.
  • Deals already closed and collected before your last day are still paid on schedule.

Your CRM

Move people through these stages

  1. New / Contacted
  2. Replied
  3. In Conversation
  4. Qualified · TPB
  5. Qualified · Academy
  6. TPB Offer Made
  7. Appointment Booked
  8. Confirmed
  9. Attended
  10. Closed-Won
  11. Nurture
  12. Disqualified

Fill these on every record

  • Source (which channel)
  • Stage (keep it current)
  • Next action + date (never leave this blank unless closed or DQ)
  • Track (TPB / Academy / Nurture)
  • Notes (why qualified or DQ)
  • Outcome + cash collected (when it closes)
If it's not in the CRM, it didn't happen. The CRM is how your work is counted and how you get paid. Keep it clean daily, not at the end of the week.

How We Show Up

Always

  • Personalized, respectful outreach
  • Answer honestly, never over-promise results
  • Follow up with care, and stop the moment someone says no
  • Treat every breeder with respect, this is a close community that talks
  • Keep your CRM accurate

Never

  • Spam, mass identical messages, or automation tools (this gets the account banned)
  • Exaggerated income or results claims
  • High-pressure tactics
  • Pushing an unqualified person onto a call
  • Ignoring an opt-out
Protect the account. Aggressive, spammy volume from one Instagram account gets it limited or banned, and that takes the whole pipeline with it. Stay personal, stay within the daily numbers, no bots. Ever.

Days 31–60 · Develop

  • Volume up to ~80% of target
  • Own all warm inbound

Days 61–90 · Produce

  • Full target volume and quality
  • Steady qualified appointments
  • Goal: hit the target scorecard two weeks running
  • Then we talk about what's next for you
