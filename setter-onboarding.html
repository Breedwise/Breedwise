Section 01

Welcome & how to use this

You were hired to do one thing exceptionally well: start real conversations with breeders and turn the right ones into members and booked calls.

Welcome to BreedWise. This playbook is your single source of truth. It explains the company, the people, the products you'll represent, and the exact way we expect the setter seat to run. Nothing here is decoration — every section maps to something you'll do in your first weeks.

How to work through it

  • Pass 1 — read it all. Front to back, once, so the whole picture clicks before you send a single DM.
  • Pass 2 — the money sections. Re-read 04 (the offer), 09 (your pay), 11 (who qualifies), and 12 (scripts) until you can explain them without looking.
  • Pass 3 — grade yourself. Finish on 16, rate your readiness 1–10 in each area, and only flag green once you'd bet your own commission on it.
One rule above all: we win by telling breeders the truth and pointing them to the right next step — not by pressure. A member who should never have joined is a refund, a bad review, and a wasted week. Qualify hard, serve honestly, and the commission follows.
Section 02

Who we are & our story

BreedWise exists to make dog breeding a real, profitable, ethical business — and to raise the standard of the whole industry while we do it.

Most breeders love their dogs and lose money. They're great at whelping and terrible at marketing, pricing, systems, and sales. They undercharge, burn out, and quit — or they cut corners that hurt the dogs and the buyers. BreedWise was built to fix that: to give serious breeders the business education, the health and genetics knowledge, the tools, and the community to run a program they're proud of and that pays.

We teach breeders to position themselves as premium, market like professionals, qualify their puppy buyers, and build repeatable income — without ever compromising on the health of the dogs or the experience of the families who buy them. "Breed wiser" is the whole thesis: better decisions, better dogs, better business.

What we've become

  • A tiered ecosystem that takes a breeder from $97/month education all the way to high-ticket done-with-you coaching.
  • A growing community (hosted on Skool) where members learn, share wins, and ascend.
  • A health & genetics arm led by veterinary expertise (reproduction, whelping, genetics) so the "wise" in BreedWise is earned, not slogan.
  • A real sales & content engine — ads, funnels, scorecards, webinars, a live summit — feeding a team that follows up and converts.
Our north star: a predictable, high-integrity sales engine that consistently moves breeders up the ladder. You are now a core part of the very top of that engine — the first human many of them talk to.

Section 05

The ascension path

A breeder rarely buys the top offer first. Your job is to meet them where they are and move them one rung at a time.

1
Cold → curious. They see our ads, reels, or a scorecard and raise their hand. They land in CC360 as a lead with a readiness tag.
2
Curious → conversation. This is you. You open the DM, qualify, and diagnose the gap between where their program is and where they want it.
3
Conversation → TPB. If they're early-stage or price-sensitive, the right next step is the $97 membership. You can set that up directly — a fast, clean win.
4
Conversation → booked call. If they're further along and serious, you don't pitch Academy/BOSS in the DM — you book the call with a closer. Your job is a qualified, confirmed, shows-up appointment.
5
Member → ascension. TPB members who get results are tomorrow's Academy and BOSS buyers. We tag readiness (academy-ready / watch / nurture) so you always know who's warm to move up.
Your lane: sell TPB directly; set Academy and BOSS. A booked call that shows and is genuinely qualified is worth more to us — and to your commission — than ten calls that no-show or never had the money.

Section 08

Your role as the setter

You are the first human in the machine. You turn attention into conversations, and conversations into the right next step.

What you own

  • Outbound DMs to breeders across our platforms — starting real conversations at volume.
  • Qualifying every prospect against our standard (Section 11) so we spend energy on real buyers.
  • Selling TPB ($97) directly in the DM when that's the right fit.
  • Setting qualified calls for Academy & BOSS — confirmed, reminded, and likely to show.
  • Logging everything in CC360 and sending a daily EOD report.

What you don't own

  • You don't close Academy/BOSS in the DM — you set them for a closer.
  • You don't quote prices you haven't confirmed, or invent discounts.
  • You're not responsible for ad spend, content, or fulfillment — but you are responsible for honest handoffs.
The mindset: you're a guide, not a pusher. Breeders can smell a script and a commission. Be genuinely curious about their program, tell them the truth about fit, and route them to the step that actually helps. That posture is also what converts.

Section 11

Who qualifies — the standard

High standards protect the breeder, the dogs, and our reputation. Money qualifies the tier — it does not qualify the person.

What we're actually screening for

  • A real program, run with integrity. Registered dogs, honest health testing, a genuine commitment to the dogs and the buyers.
  • Coachability. Someone who will do the work, not just buy access and vanish.
  • Fit for the tier. Budget tells you which rung — not whether they belong at all.

Catch the common bluffs

  • "I health test" — ask what tests, on which dogs, and for which results. Vague answers are a flag.
  • No registration — dogs that aren't registered get real scrutiny before we take them seriously.
  • Crossbreeding — scrutinized harder, not waved through because the money's there. Fit still has to be earned.
  • "Just need leads / a website" — a service request is not the same as fit for the program. Don't force it.
Don't let budget override standards. A prospect can afford BOSS and still be wrong for us. Qualifying someone out cleanly and kindly is a win — it protects the room and your own close rate. When you're unsure, flag it rather than forcing it.

Full detail lives in the Program Qualification Standard — ask Austin/Regina for the link if you need the deep version.

Section 12

Messaging & scripts

Frameworks, not robots. Learn the shape of a great conversation, then sound like a human inside it.

The shape of every conversation

1
Open — earn the reply. Personalized, specific, no pitch. Reference their content, their breed, or what they just engaged with.
2
Qualify — get them talking. Where's their program now? What do they want? What's in the way? Listen more than you type.
3
Diagnose — name the gap. Reflect back the distance between where they are and where they want to be.
4
Route — the right next step. TPB in the DM for early-stage; "let's get you on a quick call" for serious Academy/BOSS fits.
5
Confirm & log. Lock the time, set the reminder, tag it to you in CC360. The booking isn't real until it's confirmed and logged.

Starter language (make it yours)

Opener: "Hey [name] — saw you breed [breed]. Quick one: are you running this as a real business yet, or still mostly covering costs?"
Qualify: "Got it. If you could fix one thing about the business side this year — pricing, buyers, consistency — what would move the needle most for you?"
Route to TPB: "Honestly, the fastest first step for where you're at is our membership — it's $97/mo and it's exactly the playbook for that. Want me to point you to it?"
Set the call: "This is past what I can solve in DMs — you'd get way more from a quick call with our team. I've got [day/time] open. Want me to lock it in?"
Keep the brands distinct. The Profitable Breeder voice and the BOSS (high-ticket) voice are deliberately different. Don't blur them — match your tone to the tier you're pointing to, and never cross-pollinate their messaging.
Section 13

Rules of engagement

The few lines that protect the brand, the breeder, and your commission. Non-negotiable.

  • Tell the truth. No invented results, no fake scarcity, no promises we can't keep. Our edge is credibility.
  • Confirm pricing in the CRM before you quote. Never improvise a price, plan, or discount.
  • Qualify honestly. If they're not a fit, say so kindly. A bad-fit sale costs more than it earns.
  • Log everything, immediately. Credit and commission follow CC360, not memory.
  • Respect the handoff. Coordinate with closers so no lead is worked twice or double-credited.
  • Protect privacy. Handle prospects' information with care; never share it outside our systems.
  • Stay on-brand & on-platform. Follow each platform's rules; don't spam, don't mislead, don't put the accounts at risk.
  • Escalate exceptions. Big deals, weird situations, refund talk, or anything you're unsure about → Austin.
If you only remember one line: represent BreedWise the way you'd want someone representing your name and your dogs. That instinct will keep you right 99% of the time.
