FAQ

What actually happens during the 2 days?

It’s a working intensive, not a talk. Day 1 (with Austin) you diagnose your real numbers and design your pricing, value and demand. Day 2 (with Dr. Fontaine) you strengthen your whelping, neonatal and dam-care systems — then build your 12-month roadmap and a 30-day next step.

Who is this for?

Established breeders producing roughly 2–3+ litters per year who want stronger margins, qualified buyers and better systems. It’s not for first-litter breeders.

Is it live, and how do I attend?

Yes — it’s a live event held on Zoom, 11:00 AM–2:00 PM ET on September 26 & 27, 2026. Your private Zoom link is emailed to you after you register, so you can join from anywhere. Recordings are included with VIP only (lifetime access).

What’s the difference between GA and VIP?

Both get the full 2 days, working tools and live Q&A. VIP adds two implementation labs, lifetime recordings, priority questions and a private 30-minute plan review with Austin — only 10 seats.

Is this veterinary training?

Dr. Fontaine teaches veterinary-backed educational material. It does not replace advice from your own veterinarian.

What will I actually walk away with?

A 12-month profitability roadmap, a 90-day next-litter demand plan, a clearer pricing and buyer process, and veterinary-backed preparation systems. The Summit gives you a plan and the tools to run it — your results depend on your dogs, market and execution.

Refunds & transfers?

Full refund up to 14 days before the Summit; within 14 days, your payment converts to a credit toward a future BreedWise event. Seats are also transferable to another breeder anytime before the event.
