About Best Frenchies Forever

A family passion, five years in the making

My name is Russell Johnson, and I'm the owner of Best Frenchies Forever. I've been breeding French Bulldogs for five years, and I am genuinely passionate about this breed.

At Best Frenchies Forever, our focus is on quality, health, and temperament. I'm dedicated to the intense care that goes into raising Frenchies, so that every puppy is healthy, well-socialized, and ready for their forever home.

Most of all, I want our puppies to go to responsible owners and safe homes. When you bring one of our Frenchies into your life, you become part of our family, and you can reach out any time.
