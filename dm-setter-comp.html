DM Setter — Role & Compensation

Build this as a revenue-producing appointment-setting role, not just someone who answers messages. The immediate priority is response coverage: BreedWise is generating interest on Facebook and Instagram, but without consistent follow-up some of that demand is wasted. The setter owns turning comments, inbound DMs, and warm leads into qualified conversations, TPB memberships, and attended Academy/BOSS appointments.

2

Why not 10% of high-ticket revenue

10% is substantial pay for someone who sets appointments but doesn't close, deliver, or manage fulfillment. At $8,000 BOSS, 10% is $800 to the setter — on top of closer pay, delivery, refunds, and acquisition costs. Reserve the larger commission for a role that independently generates, qualifies, and closes.

The 3% is paid as installments are actually collected, not up front — keeping commission expense aligned with revenue collection. Academy in 3×$1,500 = $45/installment (up to $135). BOSS in 4×$2,000 = $60/installment (up to $240).

TPB $20 is about 20.6% of the first $97 payment — reasonable if the member stays. Split it into two $10s so the setter is incentivized to attract the right people, not drive poor-fit signups: $10 after the first payment, $10 after the second monthly payment.

5

When to hire the second setter

Don't hire because the first is booking a certain number of calls. Hire when the first is consistently at capacity and qualified demand is going unanswered. Consider expansion only when these persist for 3 consecutive weeks:

0 of 5 met. Gather the remaining evidence before committing to another setter.
Measure capacity by actual conversation complexity and coverage hours — not raw message counts. A prospect needing six exchanges, qualification, and scheduling is not the same as someone asking for a link. For the first 30 days, track how many conversations one setter handles per hour without hurting response time or appointment quality — that becomes your hiring baseline. Planning reference: ~30–40 meaningful conversations and 2–3 qualified bookings per full coverage day.

The $200/week issue to confirm first

If you expect all-day Facebook/Instagram monitoring across a full workweek, $200/week is unlikely to be an appropriate guaranteed base. Hours drive everything:

Excludes commissions. Legal requirements depend on classification, jurisdiction, total comp, actual hours, and wage/overtime rules. A commission arrangement doesn't exempt an employee from wage protections, and calling someone a contractor doesn't settle classification — the U.S. DOL emphasizes that actual working practices matter. For a first hire, prefer defined part-time coverage blocks checked against wage requirements rather than all-day availability for $200/week.
7

How to launch it

Week 1 — Establish coverage.

Set inbox responsibilities, qualification rules, CRM stages, reporting. Begin manually capturing every FB/IG sales opportunity.

Week 2 — Improve conversations.

Review transcripts, missed messages, booking quality, objection handling, follow-up. Fix the biggest leaks before adding lead volume.

Week 3 — Add automation.

ManyChat / approved Meta automations for initial responses and opt-in, with clear human handoffs and no duplicate messages. Automation reduces routine work, it doesn't replace qualified conversations.

Week 4 — Validate economics & capacity.

Review attended calls, TPB acquisitions, cash collected, commission cost, response performance, and how much coverage one setter can sustain.

★

Final recommendation

Launch with one reliable, part-time DM setter, an agreed coverage schedule, the proposed $200/week base (after hours & classification review), up to $20 per retained TPB member, and 3% of eligible Academy/BOSS cash collected. Do not pay 10% to an appointment setter unless the scope changes to independently sourced and closed revenue with margins that support it. Most importantly — set response-time and qualification standards before increasing lead generation. The one detail to confirm before publishing: how many hours per week of active coverage you expect.

The opportunity

BreedWise helps serious dog breeders improve their breeding programs and businesses through veterinary-informed education, practical tools, coaching, and implementation support. We're looking for a proactive, organized, results-driven DM Setter to manage incoming conversations, engage interested breeders, qualify prospects, and connect the right people with the appropriate BreedWise program.

The Profitable Breeder (TPB) — $97/month membership: assessments, education, tools, weekly guidance.   BreedWise Academy — structured coaching, accountability, guided implementation.   BOSS — premium, hands-on implementation support.

Your job is not to pressure people into buying. It's to engage quickly, understand their needs, create genuine interest, and move qualified prospects toward the right next step.

Your primary mission

Turn BreedWise's existing social attention into qualified conversations, paid memberships, and attended sales appointments. Success is measured by response speed, conversation quality, qualified opportunities, consistent follow-up, and attributed sales.

1 · Core responsibilities
Facebook & Instagram engagement
  • Monitor assigned FB & IG messages and respond promptly to inbound questions.
  • Engage with comments that indicate interest; initiate permitted DM conversations with relevant prospects.
  • Continue conversations generated by existing automations; avoid duplicate messages or repeated qualification.
  • Escalate customer-service, veterinary, and sensitive concerns to the appropriate team member.
Lead qualification
  • Learn the prospect's breeding experience/stage, primary challenge, desired outcome, timeline/readiness, and interest in support.
  • The goal is to identify genuine needs — not interrogate prospects.
TPB membership conversations
  • Explain TPB's value, help suitable prospects understand the membership, and direct interested breeders to the official membership page.
  • Answer approved questions on price, resources, and access; follow up appropriately on requests for more information.
Academy & BOSS appointment setting
  • Identify prospects with needs appropriate for deeper support; explain the purpose of a Breeder Game Plan Call.
  • Book qualified prospects onto approved calendars, confirm interest/availability, send authorized reminders.
  • Update the CRM with useful discovery info; coordinate with the closing team after appointments.
CRM & follow-up
  • Record meaningful conversations; maintain accurate lead ownership, status, and sales attribution.
  • Follow up per approved rules; identify inactive conversations worth revisiting; track scheduled calls and outcomes.
2 · Response & service standards

During agreed coverage windows (targets, adjusted to staffing, schedules, platform limits, and actual volume):

  • New inbound sales DMs: initial response within 15 minutes.
  • Relevant public comments: reviewed and addressed within 60 minutes.
  • Active conversations: timely follow-through, prioritized by interest and urgency; high-intent leads prioritized for immediate qualification or handoff.

The setter is responsible for the assigned coverage period, not 24/7 monitoring. Messages outside covered periods are handled in the next window. Platform messaging permissions, consent requirements, and automation restrictions must be followed.

3 · Performance expectations

Initial planning targets, validated after the first 30 days:

  • Conversation activity: ~30–40 meaningful two-way conversations per full coverage day, given sufficient qualified lead volume.
  • Qualified appointments: ~2–3 booked per full coverage day once systems and volume support it.
  • Appointment quality: 75–85% show rate (planning assumption) via confirmation and follow-up.
  • TPB sales: attributed paid memberships through qualified conversations; targets set after reviewing conversion rates and lead volume.
  • CRM accuracy: 100% of qualified opportunities, booked appointments, and attributed sales recorded accurately. No fabricated activity, misleading qualification, or duplicate commission claims.

Targets adjust proportionately for approved part-time coverage and actual inbound demand.

4 · Daily outputs

By the end of each coverage period: review assigned messages, address actionable comments, progress active conversations, complete due follow-ups, log appointments and lead status, flag high-priority opportunities, and escalate unresolved issues. A short daily report covers: new conversations initiated, meaningful conversations handled, qualified prospects, TPB memberships attributed, qualified calls booked, calls attended (where known), follow-ups completed, open high-intent leads, and issues needing management.

5 · Compensation
  • Base: $200/week, subject to scope, schedule, employment classification, and wage laws.
  • TPB: up to $20 per qualified attributed new paying member — $10 after the first $97 payment, $10 after the second monthly payment.
  • Academy: 3% of eligible, attributed net cash collected from a new enrollment in the eligibility window.
  • BOSS: 3% of eligible, attributed net cash collected from a new enrollment in the eligibility window.

High-ticket commissions are paid only as eligible payments are actually collected. The eligibility window, attribution rules, refunds, chargebacks, and timing are set in the final agreement. Booking an appointment does not itself earn commission — eligibility requires a qualifying, documented sale and collected payment.

6 · Professional standards
  • Communicate professionally; never misrepresent services or guarantee breeding, veterinary, or financial results.
  • No unauthorized discounts or payment promises; protect customer and prospect information.
  • Follow approved scripts and adapt them naturally; avoid spam, excessive follow-up, and prohibited platform activity.
  • Respect a prospect's decision to decline; keep all lead records inside approved BreedWise systems; raise problems early.
7 · First 30 days
  • Week 1 — Training & systems: learn BreedWise, offers, qualification standards, CRM, communication expectations, workflows.
  • Week 2 — Consistent execution: independently handle inboxes, qualify prospects, book calls, maintain records.
  • Week 3 — Conversion improvement: sharpen follow-up, objection handling, call quality, efficiency.
  • Week 4 — Performance review: review response times, qualified conversations, appointments, attendance, attributed sales, CRM accuracy, and workload; set realistic ongoing expectations.
8 · Advancement
  • Senior DM Setter — sustained high-quality conversion, independent ownership, reliable performance.
  • Sales Team Lead — coaches other setters, manages quality, coordinates inbox coverage.
  • Sales Manager — people leadership, pipeline management, forecasting, broader sales accountability.

Advancement is based on demonstrated performance and business needs, not tenure.

The BreedWise expectation

We're not looking for someone who sends messages and waits for instructions. We want someone who takes ownership of assigned sales conversations, identifies qualified opportunities, follows through consistently, and helps serious breeders find the support they actually need — turning conversations into meaningful opportunities and measurable revenue for BreedWise.
