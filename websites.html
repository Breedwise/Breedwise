Fair questions

Good to know.

Do I own the website?

Yes, completely. Your domain is registered to you, the site lives in your name, and your form and CRM accounts are yours. You are never locked in, and if you ever leave, everything keeps running.

Can I update my own puppies?

Yes. That is the Kennel OS. You add and edit your Available puppies, upload photos, and manage prices and status yourself — changes go live the moment you save. No waiting on us.

Do you use WordPress, Wix, or Squarespace?

No. Every site is hand-built so it loads fast, reads well on a phone, and is built around your program instead of a theme. That is why it performs and why it is truly yours.

Are the sites you show real breeders?

Yes — every site in The Work is a real, live program you can open right now. Those breeders run their own puppies through the Kennel OS.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the tier and how fast we get your photos and details. A single page can be days; a full site with the Kennel OS and systems is a few weeks. You get a timeline in writing up front.

What happens after launch?

You own and run it. We train you on the Kennel OS, and our care plan keeps hosting, updates, and support handled so you always have someone to call.
