What Should I Post?

Don't Become a BreedWise Advertisement

We invited you because people already listen to you. Keep creating the content that built that trust. When BreedWise fits naturally, talk about things like:

Your Story
"Here's what I wish I knew when I started breeding…"
Your Experience
"This is something that changed the way we run our breeding program…"
What You've Learned
"One of the things I learned through BreedWise was…"
Your Results
"Here's what changed for our program after we started doing this differently…"
Your Recommendation
"If you're a breeder struggling with this too, here's where I'd recommend starting…"

Then share your BreedWise link.

Share what happened for you. Never promise what will happen for someone else.
