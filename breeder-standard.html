The BreedWise Breeder Standard

The Standard
We Hold.

Better Your Breed. Build a Better Business.

At BreedWise, we believe the foundation of a successful breeding business is being a responsible breeder. Profitability is important — but it should never come at the expense of the dogs in our care, the integrity of our breeding decisions, or the trust of the families who purchase our puppies.

Responsible breeding practices, ethical business decisions, and continuous improvement belong together. That is why every member of The Profitable Breeder, BreedWise Academy, and BOSS is expected to uphold this standard.

You do not have to be a perfect breeder to belong here. But you must be committed to doing things the right way, being accountable for your decisions, and continually improving.

Our Non-Negotiable Position

What BreedWise does not tolerate

    Serious or repeated violations may result in restricted access, suspension, or removal from BreedWise programs, subject to our member review and enforcement process.
    The BreedWise Commitment

    My commitment as a member

    As a BreedWise member, I acknowledge that responsible breeding, animal welfare, ethical business practices, and professional integrity are essential to the quality of the breeding industry.

    I understand that BreedWise membership is a privilege accompanied by responsibilities. I commit to maintaining high standards, being accountable for my decisions, and making meaningful efforts to improve my breeding program and business.

    I understand that BreedWise may review credible concerns about member conduct and take appropriate action under its published policies.

    I don't have to know everything. But I commit to putting my dogs first, acting with integrity, and continually becoming a better breeder.
