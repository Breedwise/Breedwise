How It Works

The Process

Bringing home a Blessed Beyond Doberman is a relationship, not a transaction. Here is exactly how it works, from the first message to the day your companion comes home, and beyond.

Step By Step

From inquiry to home

Step 01

Reach Out

Send us an inquiry and tell us about your family, your home, and the companion you are hoping for. We reply quickly, every day from 8am to 8pm MT, and we will answer any questions honestly.

Step 02

Get Acquainted

We talk, by phone or message, to make sure a Doberman and this particular litter are the right fit for your life. This is where we match the right puppy to the right family.

Step 03

Reserve Your Place

A deposit secures your spot on the list. Reservations choose in the order they are placed, so the earlier you reserve, the more choice you have when the puppies arrive.

Step 04

Through The Journey

We keep you close through the pregnancy, the whelp, and those first weeks, with updates and photos as the litter grows and temperaments begin to show.

Step 05

Choose & Prepare

When the time comes, you select your puppy in your reserved order. We send a full go-home guide so your house is ready before pickup or delivery day.

Step 06

Welcome Home

Your well-started, socialized puppy comes home, by pickup or careful nationwide delivery, with vaccinations, paperwork, a go-home kit, and our number for life.

Good To Know

Frequently asked

How much is a puppy and what is the deposit?

Our puppies start at $5,500, depending on the litter and placement. A deposit reserves your place on the list and is applied to the total. Reach out and we will walk you through the current litter and what is available.

Are the parents health tested?

Yes. Every parent is Embark DNA tested and health screened. We breed for longevity and soundness, and results are available to you. A written health guarantee comes home with your puppy.

Do you ship, and how?

We place puppies with families nationwide and arrange careful delivery when you cannot pick up in person. We will discuss the safest option for your location and timeline, and we never compromise on a puppy's comfort in transit.

What comes home with the puppy?

Age-appropriate vaccinations and deworming, a vet wellness check, AKC registration paperwork, a written health guarantee, a starter supply of food, a comfort item from the litter, and a detailed care guide.

Do you dock tails and crop ears?

We are happy to talk through your preferences and what we offer. These are personal decisions and we will give you honest guidance based on twelve years with the breed.

What support do I get after I bring the puppy home?

All of it, for the life of your dog. Training questions, nutrition, health, behavior, anything at all. Our families keep our number and we genuinely love hearing how our puppies grow up.

American or European Dobermans?

We can talk through the differences and which of our lines may suit your lifestyle best. Temperament and fit come first in every recommendation we make.
