Questions

Good to know

A few of the things families ask us most.

Is the Boerboel right for first-time owners?

Boerboels are intelligent, dominant, and deeply devoted, they thrive with confident owners who provide structure, socialisation, and leadership. We're honest about fit, and we help you decide.

How do you place puppies?

By application, not first-come. We talk with every family and match temperament to household.

What does a puppy cost, and what's the deposit?

Pricing varies by litter and is shared with approved applicants. A deposit reserves your place and is applied to the balance.

Do you ship or deliver?

Most families collect their puppy in person here in Milton, Georgia. For the right home, we are glad to discuss safe travel arrangements.

Do you offer any health guarantee?

Our breeding dogs are health-tested (OFA, Embark, Animal Genetics), and every puppy goes home with its health records and our written contract. We are always glad to talk through the details.

Where are you located?

Milton, Georgia. Visits are by appointment for approved applicants.

Start with an application. We'll take it one honest step at a time.
