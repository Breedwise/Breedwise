The Profitable Breeder

New Member → Academy Ascension SOP

Version 1.0 · Owners: Jennifer → Regina → Austin · Objective: move every TPB member to the right next step with maximum clarity, useful data, and qualified Academy opportunities at minimum member friction.
The operating principle: Jennifer discovers the need. Regina qualifies the opportunity. Austin diagnoses deeper and closes. Jennifer owns implementation after enrollment. The CRM is the source of truth — conversations happen elsewhere, but decisions and next actions live in the CRM. No member should ever have to re-explain their situation to BreedWise.
01

Non-Negotiable Rules

§41
  1. Never make a member repeat information BreedWise already has.
  2. Never surprise someone with the $6,000 Academy price on Austin's call.
  3. Never send Austin an unqualified call just to fill his calendar.
  4. Never pitch Academy simply because someone has a low Scorecard score.
  5. Never let a sales opportunity exist without an owner, next action, and date.
  6. Never use Slack / text / DMs as the permanent customer record.
  7. Never leave "Not Yet" without a reactivation trigger and date.
  8. Never have Jennifer turn every Member Success conversation into a sales conversation.
  9. Never let Regina prescribe solutions she is supposed to qualify.
  10. Never have Austin restart discovery from zero.

03

Member Requirements & Actions

§2, 6, 9, 20

The member completes only three primary information events before an Academy sales call. Do not add another Academy application unless future data proves it necessary.

#1 · TPB Onboarding

Target 3–5 min

Who they are, where their program stands, current challenges, desired outcome.

#2 · Profitable Breeder Scorecard

Target ~10 min

Creates the baseline: Strongest Area → Biggest Gap → #1 Priority.

#3 · Academy Pre-Call Confirmation

Target ~60 sec

What to implement → why now → decision process → $6K investment awareness.

§6Member Action #1 — Onboarding questions

Breeding program

  • What breed(s) do you work with?
  • How long have you been breeding? (haven't had first litter · <1yr · 1–3 · 4–7 · 8+)
  • Litters in the last 12 months?
  • Litters planned in the next 12 months?
  • When is your next planned breeding or litter?

Business & demand

  • Current average puppy price?
  • Which best describes demand? (not enough inquiries · plenty but unqualified · qualified but not converting · usually enough · waitlist before breeding)

Challenges

  • Biggest challenge in your breeding program right now?
  • Biggest challenge on the business side right now?

Outcome & contact

  • If the next 90 days went extremely well, what would be different?
  • Phone · Email · Instagram

§9Member Action #2 — Scorecard

Completed across: 1) Positioning · 2) Audience & Demand · 3) Waitlist · 4) Inquiries & Sales · 5) Pricing · 6) Litter Economics · 7) Breeding Systems & Preparation. Finishes with My strongest area / My biggest gap / My #1 priority.

Lowest score ≠ automatic priority. Jennifer weighs score + member goal + timing + upcoming breeding/litter + impact + urgency. The Scorecard establishes the baseline; it does not automatically prescribe the solution.

§20Member Action #3 — Pre-Call Confirmation (~60s)

  • What's the #1 thing you want help implementing or improving right now?
  • Why is solving this important to you now?
  • Who besides you, if anyone, needs to be involved in the decision?
"I understand BreedWise Academy is a $6,000 implementation program, available as $6,000 upfront or 4 payments of $1,500, and I'm open to discussing enrollment if it's the right fit." ☐ Yes

No 20-question application.

§5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12

§5Stage 1 — New Member

  • Trigger: successful $97 TPB purchase. Owner: Jennifer. SLA: personal welcome within 1 business day.
  • Automation immediately: create CRM record · send access info · assign Stage "TPB — New Member" + Owner Jennifer · send onboarding.

§7Onboarding follow-up

  • Day 2: reminder · Day 5: personal Jennifer follow-up · Day 7: mark ONBOARDING INCOMPLETE
  • Jennifer personally investigates rather than endlessly automating reminders. This is an early member-health signal.

§8Jennifer's personal welcome

Review onboarding before messaging. Never send the identical "personalized" message to everyone.

Jennifer then changes stage → TPB — Scorecard.

§10Activation gate

A member becomes TPB — ACTIVATED only when all four are true:

  • Onboarding complete
  • Scorecard complete
  • Strongest area identified
  • #1 priority established

Jennifer records the priority. Now TPB delivers its promise: know where you stand → understand the gaps → know what to improve next.

§11Jennifer's ascension role

Jennifer does not ask "Who can I sell Academy to?" She asks: "Who has moved from needing clarity to needing implementation?" That is the ascension trigger.

Academy signals

  • Implementation need — "I know what to change, not how to build it."
  • Complexity — "I don't know how to put this all together."
  • Accountability — "I need someone keeping me on track."
  • Feedback — "I want someone to look at what I've built."
  • Urgency — upcoming breeding, litter, launch, price increase, transition.
  • Established activity — enough real activity for support to matter.
  • Meaningful impact — materially affects program, time, buyers, margin, confidence.

NOT a signal by itself

  • Being engaged
  • Having money
  • Posting frequently
  • A low Scorecard score
  • Asking questions
  • Being a breeder a long time

Ascension requires an identifiable implementation need.

Regina — Qualify & Route

§15–19

§15Regina's SLA

  • Accept the handoff same business day when possible. First contact within 1 business day.
  • No response: Attempt 1 (initial) · Attempt 2 (+1 day) · Attempt 3 (+3 days) · Attempt 4 (+7 days). Then disposition NO RESPONSE, set a reactivation date if appropriate, return ownership to Jennifer. Do not chase indefinitely.

§16Regina's opening

Prove immediately that BreedWise communicates internally.

Hey Sarah, Jennifer filled me in before I reached out. I saw you're preparing for your spring litter, and one of the biggest things you've identified is building stronger qualified demand before puppies arrive. I just want to understand that a little better and see what the right next step is for you.

Never open with "So tell me about your breeding program." BreedWise already knows.

§17Qualification call (~7–10 min) — six answers

  • Pain — What's specifically happening with [priority/problem] right now?
  • Impact — How is that affecting your program or business?
  • Attempts — What have you already tried to improve it?
  • Implementation need — Mainly need information and a plan, or do you understand what to change and want hands-on help implementing it?
  • Urgency — Why is fixing this important now? What happens if nothing changes in 6–12 months?
  • Readiness — If Academy is the right fit, are you in a position to invest $6,000 (upfront or payment plan)?

No pressure. No surprise. No hiding price until Austin's call.

§18Academy Qualification gate

A lead becomes ACADEMY — QUALIFIED only when Regina confirms all eight:

  • Problem — identifiable problem exists
  • Impact — matters enough to solve
  • Implementation need — needs more than info
  • Urgency — legitimate reason to act
  • Program fit — Academy can support it
  • Investment awareness — knows it's $6,000
  • Readiness — open to investing if appropriate
  • Decision process — knows who must participate

If those aren't established, do not book Austin "to see what happens."

§19Regina's three routes

A · Qualified

Book Austin immediately.

B · Not Yet

Fit, but timing/readiness isn't there. Set Reason + Trigger + Reactivation Date (e.g. after current litter placed → Dec 1).

C · TPB

Needs education/clarity/fundamentals, not implementation. Return ownership to Jennifer. No awkward rejection.

Austin — Diagnose & Close

§22–25, 28

§22–23Opening & role

Start from accumulated context, not from zero.

Sarah, I've reviewed your onboarding, Scorecard, and Regina's notes before we got on. It looks like you've got three litters planned over the next year, and while you're generating inquiries, the real issue is having enough qualified families ready before puppies arrive. With your next breeding coming up, you don't want to repeat the same cycle. Is that an accurate picture?

If yes, go deeper. Austin owns: Validate → Deepen → Diagnose → Fit → Prescribe → Offer → Objections → Close. Not re-asking everything Regina already asked. Austin determines whether Academy is appropriate and presents it when it is; the member decides whether they buy.

§24Call outcomes — exactly one CRM disposition

  • WON — enrollment completed
  • FOLLOW-UP — unresolved decision + exact next step
  • NOT YET — fit, but timing/readiness isn't
  • NOT FIT — Academy isn't appropriate
  • LOST — qualified, chose not to proceed
  • NO-SHOW — didn't attend

There is no "thinking about it." If they're thinking: what specifically are they deciding, what happens next, who owns it, when?

§25Standard Lost / Not-Yet reasons (dropdowns, not notes)

Timing · Investment · Partner/Decision Maker · Insufficient Urgency · Needs TPB First · Not Implementation Ready · Program Not Appropriate · No Response · No-Show · Chose Alternative · Breeding Plans Changed · Other. Free-text supplements the disposition; it cannot replace it.

11

Follow-Up, No-Show & Won

§26, 27, 29, 30, 31

§26Follow-up rule

Every open opportunity must have OWNER + NEXT ACTION + DATE (e.g. Regina · contact after spouse discussion · Oct 9). If any of the three is blank, the opportunity is operationally broken.

§27No-show SOP

  • +15 min: Regina contacts — "Hey Sarah, looks like we missed you for your call with Austin. Everything okay?"
  • Same day: send rescheduling option · +1 day: follow up · +3 days: final active follow-up
  • Then NO-SHOW — assign a reactivation date or return to Jennifer.

§29Won SOP

Regina verifies:

  • Agreement signed
  • Payment received / plan established
  • Correct CRM amount
  • Cash collected recorded
  • Start date recorded
  • Sales notes complete
  • Jennifer notified

Pipeline → ACADEMY — WON. Ownership returns to Jennifer.

§30Jennifer's Academy welcome — within 1 business day

Sarah! I just saw the update from Austin — welcome to Academy 🎉 I've already got your TPB onboarding, Scorecard, Regina's notes, and the context from your conversation with Austin, so we're not starting from scratch. Our next step is to establish your Academy baseline and turn what we've already learned into your personalized 120-day implementation roadmap. I'll send you exactly what to complete next.

Members should feel BreedWise's knowledge of them compounds.

§31Academy handoff

Jennifer begins the Academy journey: Assess → Prioritize → Implement → Measure → Reassess. The Academy baseline pulls forward existing TPB information wherever possible. Do not ask "What breed do you have?" again — only collect what's required for implementation and doesn't already exist.

§38Three numbers to watch closely

1 · TPB → Activated %

If bad, don't blame sales — the onboarding/Member Success system is leaking.

2 · Qualified Austin call %

If Austin keeps finding people who aren't serious/ready/aware, Regina's qualification is leaking.

3 · Qualified call → cash

Genuinely qualified people showing up but not enrolling → Austin's sales process is leaking.

Accountability without people blaming one another.

§39–40Weekly 20-min meeting & daily check

Weekly 20 min — Jen + Regina + Austin

  • 0–5: numbers (new, activated, signals, qualified, booked, showed, won, cash)
  • 5–15: only members needing a decision — where are they, what's blocking, who's next, by when?
  • 15–20: one question — where did the ascension process break this week? Choose one improvement. End.

Not a general company meeting.

Daily ownership check

  • Jennifer — which members need help moving forward?
  • Regina — which opportunities need a conversation or next action today?
  • Austin — which qualified conversations require my involvement?
