1Golden Rules

One pipeline. One record.

All sales work happens in CC360, in a single pipeline. One lead = one record, one place to change stages.

Lead = raised hand only

A "lead" is someone who has:

  • Booked a call, or
  • Submitted an application, or
  • DM'd / replied with clear buying intent — "I want help with my breeding program," "How do I apply?"
👥 New followers, likes, and casual DMs = audience, not leads. They live in CC360 as audience and never enter the pipeline. This one rule is what keeps every number honest.

CC360 is the brain

CC360
The only place stages change. The operational CRM + pipeline.
Close
Dialer + call log only. No deals, no stages.
Sales OS
Scoreboard only. Reporting, attribution & comp.

Every lead has a source

Exactly one source tag per lead, so we always know what's working:

ad-casestudyig-dmig-organicvsl-applicationreferral

We can add more later — but it's always exactly 1 per lead.

2The 9 Pipeline Stages

All opportunities live in one CC360 pipeline with these stages:

New
Raised-hand lead just createdApp, booked call, or DM with intent. No human has touched it yet.
Working
Outreach has startedSetter/closer is working it — calls, texts, emails. Not yet applied or booked.
App
Application submittedApplied, but not yet booked for a Gameplan Call.
Booked
Gameplan & Enrollment Call on the calendarLead has received confirmation email/text.
Show
Attended the Gameplan Call (≥ 15 min)Call complete; next step is decision or follow-up.
No-Show
Booked but did not attendTriggers reactivation / reschedule attempts.
Won
Enrolled + made initial paymentNow belongs to Ops/Success for onboarding.
Lost
Clear "no" or not a fitNo further sales work planned.
Nurture
Not ready now, worth staying in touchTiming or funds. Worked by long-term nurture / scheduled outbound later.

3Who Owns What

Marketing CC360

Drives traffic to VSL, Playbook, IG. Ensures every form/opt-in:

  • Creates/updates the contact in CC360
  • Applies exactly one source tag
  • Only creates an opportunity when the lead raises a hand

Setter Close + CC360

  • Works New and Working stages.
  • Calls/texts from Close, updates stage in CC360 only.
  • Books Gameplan Calls → moves to Booked.
  • Runs show-up / reminder sequences. Owns show rate on their sets.

Closer Close + CC360

  • Runs Gameplan & Enrollment Calls.
  • After every call, moves the lead to Show, No-Show, Won, or Lost.
  • Adds notes + next steps in CC360.
  • Owns close rate and cash collected from Shows.

Ops / Success Member OS

  • Takes over after Won.
  • Runs onboarding and fulfillment.
  • Feeds back proof, churn drivers, and client stories.

Sales OS Leadership only

Reads events from CC360 + Close. Shows daily/weekly numbers — leads, apps, booked, shows, won, cash; show rate, close rate; dials & talk time per rep. Drives comp and decisions. No reps work here day-to-day.

4Daily Flow

Lead raises hand → CC360 creates contact + source tag → New → Setter works it → Working → qualifies + books → Booked → reminders → Show / No-Show → Closer runs call → Won / Lost / Nurture
  1. Lead raises hand → CC360 creates/updates contact + applies one source tag → New.
  2. Setter pulls from New → starts outreach → Working.
  3. Setter qualifies + books → moves to Booked.
  4. Setter runs the reminder sequence → lead Shows or is No-Show.
  5. Closer runs the call: enrolls → Won; otherwise Lost or Nurture (with a reason).
  6. Sales OS ingests every event → updates the scoreboard.
