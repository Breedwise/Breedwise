Before You Apply

What happens after I apply?
We personally review every application. If it looks like a fit, we'll reach out to schedule your free Breeding Program & Enrollment Call. If it's not a fit right now, we'll let you know — no wasted time on either side.
Is the call free?
Yes. The call is free and designed to help you make a clear yes/no decision about working together. We audit your program, identify your biggest bottleneck, and give you 3–5 tactical next steps either way.
Do I need to be breeding already?
You should already be breeding, or actively preparing to whelp your first litters. This is for breeders ready to build a real, ethical program — not people just "curious" about getting started.
How much does the Academy cost?
Our programs range from $1,997–$15,000 depending on the level of support. We'll only talk specifics on the call if you're qualified and excited about the plan. Nothing to buy on this page.
Will you hard-sell me?
No. On the call we map your situation, find your biggest bottleneck, and walk you through what we'd do. If it's a fit, we'll explain how Breedwise works and the investment. If not, you'll still leave with clarity on your next steps.
