Start here — everyone

The Member Success OS is a live dashboard of member health, retention, community engagement, pods, issues, and your pay. Every number is real and updates the moment anyone edits a member.

Logging in

  1. Go to review.breedwise.io/team
  2. Sign in with your Breedwise email + password (Austin sets these — ask him if you don't have one).
  3. You'll land on your dashboard. The tabs you can act on depend on your role, but everyone can see the whole picture.
🔒 Your pay is private. The database is locked so no teammate can read your compensation — only you, and Austin as Founder.

The tabs

Company Health
Top-level KPIs (churn, activation, NPS, failed payments), the member funnel, and the full member list.
Pod Command Center
Per-pod attendance, homework, and churn — for Accountability Leaders.
Community Engine
Week-1 activation, ongoing attendance, engagement, and the "needs attention" queue.
Issues
Log anything a member or the community is struggling with — tag urgency + a due date.
My Pay
Your private compensation breakdown (Founder sees the whole team).
Automation
The rules that auto-color members and turn events into owned tasks.
System Spec
The exact KPI formulas, activation steps, and the permissions matrix.

How member status works

Every member is auto-colored from their data. You can override it per member if you know better.

  • Green healthy — activated, ≥3 calls in 4 weeks, homework on track, payment good.
  • Yellow drifting — missed activation, 0 calls, no posts, or a failed payment.
  • Red at risk — NPS ≤ 6, 30+ days silent, cancelled, or a manual escalation.

Updating a member

  1. On Company Health (or your pod), click a member's Edit — or + Add member for a new one.
  2. Enter what's current: calls (last 4 wks), posts (7 days), homework %, NPS, payment status, and flags (activated, new, churned, failed payment).
  3. Save. Their status color and everyone's KPIs recalculate instantly.

Logging an issue

  1. Open the Issues tab → + Log an issue.
  2. Add a short summary, pick the member (optional), set urgency (low → urgent) and a due date, and write context.
  3. Overdue items flag in red and sort to the top. Hit Resolve when it's handled.

Director Jennifer

You own overall member success — keeping members happy, activated, and paying.

Your home tab

Company Health. The four KPIs at the top are your scoreboard; the member list below is your worklist.

Your KPIs & targets

Monthly churn
cancels ÷ members · target < 3%
Activation rate
activated ÷ new · target ≥ 80%
NPS
% promoters − % detractors · target > 80
Failed payments
members w/ failed pay ÷ active · target < 3%

Daily rhythm

  • Scan Company Health — anything trending the wrong way?
  • Work every Red member first; then Yellows drifting toward Red.
  • Clear urgent & overdue items in the Issues tab.
  • Keep member records current so the KPIs stay honest.

What you can do

Full control: create / edit / delete members, override any member's status, and manage pods & assignments.

How your pay works

See it under My Pay. Base salary plus a bonus for each of the four KPIs that's on target — the breakdown shows exactly which ones you hit.

Accountability Leader Derek

You own your pod — their attendance, their homework, and keeping them from churning.

Your home tab

Pod Command Center. Pick your pod to see its KPIs and member list.

Your KPIs & targets

Pod attendance
pod on ≥1 call ÷ active pod · target ≥ 70%
Homework
items done ÷ assigned · target ≥ threshold
Pod churn
pod cancels ÷ pod active · target < 3%

Daily rhythm

  • Open your pod — who has 0 calls or low homework? Reach out today.
  • Catch failed/at-risk payments early and flag the Director.
  • Log anything a member is struggling with in Issues.

How your pay works

Base per active member in your pod, plus a pod bonus based on hitting your metrics (a ramp scale in your first ~60 days, then ongoing), plus an upsell kicker on Accelerator/Elite revenue closed in your pod. The full math is in My Pay and System Spec.

Community Manager Madison

You own the community — getting new members activated fast, keeping them showing up, and triaging issues.

Your home tab

Community Engine. The "needs attention" queue auto-surfaces who's slipping.

Your KPIs & targets

Week-1 activation
on both week-1 calls ÷ new · target ≥ 80%
Ongoing attendance
≥1 call in 3 of 4 weeks ÷ active · target ≥ 70%
Engagement
meaningful posts ÷ active ÷ week · target ≥ 1

Daily rhythm

  • Make sure every new member hits both week-1 calls — that's the biggest retention lever.
  • Work the "needs attention" queue: silent members, no posts.
  • Log & triage member issues (this is baseline — expected, every day).

How your pay works

Base plus a bonus for each KPI on target. Issue triage is part of the baseline role (required, not a bonus). See My Pay.

Founder Austin

You see everything — including every person's compensation (no one else can see anyone's but their own).

  • My Pay → Team Pay: full compensation for the whole team, computed live from their KPIs.
  • Every other tab is full-access oversight: company health, every pod, the community engine, and all issues.
  • Use it to spot where to coach — a Red-heavy pod, a sliding KPI, a pile of overdue issues.

Quick answers

  • Numbers look stale? Refresh the page — it always reads the live database.
  • Forgot your password? Ask Austin to reset it.
  • Can teammates see my pay? No. The database physically blocks it.
  • Edited a member but a KPI didn't move? KPIs recompute on save and reload — give it a refresh.
