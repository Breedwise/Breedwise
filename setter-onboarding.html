Part 1 · Section 01

Welcome, purpose & expectations

Welcome, Daniel. This manual is built so you can run your seat with minimal supervision — log in, work the inbox, book calls, recover old leads, and report, without needing Austin for routine questions.

What your work does for BreedWise

You are often the first human a breeder talks to. You turn attention into real conversations, and real conversations into the right next step. Growth depends on this seat being fast, honest, and consistent.

Know the difference between these four things

ConversationA real back-and-forth with a prospect.
Qualified prospectA breeder with a genuine need and a suitable next step.
Booked appointmentA confirmed calendar slot with full details — not just "sure, I'd talk."
Attended callThe prospect actually showed up. This is what counts.
Core principle. We do not measure success by how many people receive a pitch. We measure it by the quality of conversations, qualified appointments generated, calls attended, and opportunities recovered. Honest qualification protects the breeder and the company — a forced call wastes everyone's time.

How you'll be trained & evaluated

Read this manual, train on the real systems, pass a manager-observed certification (Section 19), then ramp on supervised live conversations (Section 18). You go live by demonstrated competence, not by the calendar.

Who BreedWise is

Better Your Breed. Build a Better Business.

BreedWise helps serious dog breeders do two things at once: raise the standard of their breeding — practices, preparation, education, and animal welfare — and build a sustainable business around it through better systems, marketing, buyer relationships, and financial awareness. "Breed wiser": better decisions, better dogs, better business.

Who our community is for

Breeders who want to improve their program and run it like a real, ethical business — whether they're established or preparing to start. We meet them where they are and point them to the right support. We don't make sweeping claims about breeders in general; we focus on fit.

Our values

Truthfulness Responsible breeding Veterinary-informed education Customer fit Professionalism Accountability

Why this matters to your seat: every message you send represents those values. You can be warm, honest, and genuinely helpful and still book more calls — in fact, that's exactly what books more calls.

Part 2 · Section 05

Your exact responsibilities

Create the right next conversation — don't finish the sale in the inbox.

1 · Booked Call

Breeder schedules & confirms a Breeder Game Plan Call with Regina — she qualifies, then passes to Austin or TPB.

2 · Qualified Callback

They consent to be contacted by the sales team when booking now isn't practical — you capture details.

3 · TPB or Nurture

Better suited to TPB or a resource than a high-ticket call — you refer, you don't hard-sell.

Do not force a call when there's no meaningful reason for one.

What you own

  • Assigned Instagram & Facebook inbox conversations, and comments that show genuine interest.
  • Approved proactive outreach to relevant engaged prospects, and re-engaging previous unanswered leads.
  • Understanding each prospect's situation and desired outcome; judging whether a call fits.
  • Inviting qualified prospects to a Breeder Game Plan Call; collecting consented callback details.
  • Booking & confirming real appointments; recording lead details & next actions in CC360; completing follow-ups; accurate reporting.

What you don't own

  • High-ticket closing, or explaining/pitching Academy & BOSS details. Extended objection handling.
  • Selling TPB as a closer — you refer to TPB; you don't run a TPB sales pitch or carry a TPB sales quota.
  • Veterinary diagnosis/treatment; financial or revenue guarantees; changing prices or terms; unsupported claims.
  • Unapproved mass messaging or automation; existing-member fulfillment.
The one-line job: start genuine conversations, identify the breeder's primary challenge and desired outcome, determine the right next step, and convert qualified interest into a confirmed call or consented callback.
Part 3 · Section 06 · ★ Priority initiative

First shift: recover missed opportunities

BreedWise is likely sitting on real opportunities — old DMs, Facebook comments, Instagram replies, and scorecard leads that never got a proper human response. Recovering them can beat hundreds of cold messages.

Backfill workflow

1
Get approved access to Instagram, Facebook, Meta Business Suite, and CC360.
2
Review existing inboxes & comment threads before launching large new outreach.
3
Identify messages/comments showing real interest (breeding education, business improvement, buyer acquisition, support).
4
Check whether a teammate or automation already answered; check for an existing customer or assigned CRM owner.
5
Prioritize unanswered, recent, high-intent leads. Re-engage using the original context.
6
Update or create the CRM record: source, last interaction, status, owner, next action.
7
Route qualified prospects toward a booking or a consented callback.

Priority order

P1New inbound questions & urgent, high-intent replies
P2Prospects already in active sales conversations
P3Interested people who got a booking link but never scheduled
P4Old unanswered DMs & relevant comments
P5Approved proactive outreach to warm, engaged prospects
Never let backfill starve the live queue. Current incoming leads come first (Section 08 speed-to-lead); backfill is worked when the live queue is clear.

Rules for older conversations

  • Don't pretend the conversation happened recently — acknowledge a delay naturally when appropriate.
  • Don't send the same generic message to every old contact — use their original context.
  • Don't contact anyone who opted out; don't reopen resolved support issues as sales conversations.
  • Respect platform restrictions and consent requirements.
Measured separately (first 30 days): Historical leads reviewed → eligible re-engaged → conversations restarted → qualified calls booked → calls attended → sales attributed. This tells us whether the recovery effort is paying — track it on its own, not mixed into cold outreach.

Part 3 · Section 07

The daily operating process

Your start-to-finish checklist. Same order, every shift.

Beginning of shift

  • Confirm assigned coverage time; open approved inboxes & CC360.
  • Check newly assigned leads; review active conversations & callbacks; review due follow-ups; note any leads owned by someone else.

During the shift

  • Answer active inbound first. Personalize using the prospect's actual context.
  • Ask only the questions needed to find the next step. Present the call invitation when there's a genuine fit; collect consented callback details when that's better.
  • Confirm appointments in the real booking system. Record meaningful updates immediately.
  • Work assigned backfill & approved outreach when the live queue is clear.

End of shift

  • Update all worked leads; complete or reassign time-sensitive follow-ups.
  • Flag missing info or technical issues; check upcoming appointments & confirmations.
  • Send the EOD report; note anything the next shift needs to know.
As-you-go rule: log each outcome the moment it happens — the CRM is your memory and your paycheck. If it isn't in CC360, it didn't happen.

Part 4 · Section 09

The DM conversation framework

One framework, adapted to how the prospect entered. It guides your judgment — it does not replace it. Never fire eight canned messages in a row.

1
Open — start naturally. "Are you currently breeding, or just getting started?" · New follower: "Hey, thanks for the follow — here to get better results for your program, or just the content?"
2
Understand — "What breed are you focused on? How long have you been breeding?" Don't ask what they've already answered.
3
Identify their focus — better breeding practices, business operations, or both?
4
Ask permission — "Do you mind if I ask a couple quick questions to see if we can help?"
5
Main struggle — "What's the biggest thing holding you back right now?" (personalize to what they mentioned).
6
Desired outcome — "If you could improve one thing over the next 3–6 months, what would you want to see change?"
7
Assess readiness — do they actually want help, and does a call make sense now? (Not an interrogation about their finances.)
8
Invite to the call — see the Game Plan Call script below.
The Breeder Game Plan Call invite: "Based on what you shared about [problem] and wanting to [goal], I think a quick conversation could help. We do a 15–20 minute Breeder Game Plan Call to look at your situation, figure out what deserves attention first, and see whether BreedWise is the right fit. Open to a call this week?"

Rules for the framework

  • Never mechanically send eight messages; ask one relevant question at a time and acknowledge the answer.
  • Don't repeat a question they've answered; avoid unnecessary emotional probing.
  • If a qualified prospect asks to talk, move to booking early — don't stretch the script.
  • Keep it conversational and concise. Only promise a personalized plan/deliverable if the call team actually provides it.

Part 4 · Section 11

Qualification & discernment

Recognize fit — without making decisions outside your expertise. Gather facts; refer the judgment calls.

Ask yourself

  • Are they genuinely breeding, preparing to breed, or seeking relevant education?
  • What's their primary challenge, and what outcome do they want?
  • Do they want help now, or just gathering info? Is a high-ticket sales conversation appropriate?
  • Any welfare or integrity concerns that need escalation?

Three routing outcomes

RouteWhen
A · Book a Game Plan CallMeaningful problem + appropriate goal + genuine interest in help.
B · Collect details & arrange a callbackWelcomes direct contact but can't/won't schedule right now.
C · Route to TPB or an educational stepA lower-touch resource fits better, or they're not ready for a high-ticket call.
Don't make categorical judgments based solely on breed, registration status, crossbreeding, or geography. Follow the company's documented qualification standard (Appendix D) and refer uncertain or sensitive cases — welfare concerns, veterinary questions, disputed situations — for review. TPB is not an inferior destination, and not everyone must climb through every offer.
Part 5 · Section 12

Booking calls properly

"Sure, I'd like to talk" is not a booked appointment. A booking is real only when it's on the calendar with full details.

The booking process

  1. Get agreement to speak.
  2. Offer real available times or the approved booking link.
  3. Confirm the time zone.
  4. Collect the necessary contact details.
  5. Schedule it in the approved system.
  6. Verify the calendar entry exists.
  7. Confirm the prospect received the invitation.
  8. Explain the call's purpose & duration (15–20 min Game Plan Call).
  9. Send the approved pre-call resource / application if required.
  10. Record qualification context & next owner in CC360.

A booked appointment has

  • Correct contact info · confirmed date/time · correct call owner · qualification notes · recorded lead source · a clear next action.
The call flow: you book the prospect onto the Breeder Game Plan Call with Regina (the application asks qualifying questions). Regina runs that call to confirm fit → if qualified, she passes them to Austin for the closing call; if not a high-ticket fit, she routes them to TPB. Record Regina as the call owner on the booking.
Never invent available times or imply a call is confirmed before it's on the calendar.
Part 5 · Section 13

Follow-ups, confirmations & reengagement

Most of the value is in the follow-up — done with respect and proportion, never pressure.

SituationApproved action
Interested prospect stops replyingBrief, context-specific check-in in an appropriate window. No pressure.
Agreed to a call but didn't bookFollow up on the scheduling step; make booking easy.
Chose a time but no contact detailsRequest only what's needed to finalize.
Got an invite but didn't confirmVerify delivery and that the time still works.
Upcoming appointmentApproved reminder cadence; avoid duplicate messages.
Missed appointmentOffer a reasonable reschedule, coordinated with the call owner.
Older unanswered leadAcknowledge the prior inquiry naturally; check if the need is still live.
"Not interested / stop contacting me"Respect it, mark the contact, discontinue outreach.
Boundaries: no aggressive scarcity, deposit pressure, excessive confirmations, or manipulative language. No prolonged objection handling or negotiating financial commitments — that's the call team's job. Keep reminders useful and proportionate.
Part 5 · Section 14

CRM procedures & attribution

CC360 is the system of record. Clean records are how you get credit and how the team wins.

You must be able to

  • Find a record · spot duplicates · create a record when needed · record lead source · document the first meaningful conversation · add qualification notes · assign ownership · update pipeline stage · set a follow-up task · record bookings & attendance · route to Regina or the call owner · mark TPB referral/purchase when verified · preserve history when re-engaging old leads.

Pipeline stages

New Lead → Contacted → Engaged → Qualified → Call Invited → Booked → Attended → Outcome Recorded

Plus statuses: Callback requested · TPB referred · Nurture · Not qualified · No-show / reschedule · Opted out. Austin/ops: define exact stage meanings & what event triggers each change (Appendix B).

Attribution

Use approved CRM source & owner rules — don't infer ownership from whoever messaged most recently. Define credit when: a lead came from marketing · you qualify it · Regina handles the phone call · Austin closes Academy/BOSS · an old lead is recovered via backfill · an existing customer buys again.

Important: this manual explains how attribution is tracked. The signed compensation agreement determines when commissions are actually earned and paid.
Part 6 · Section 15

Performance metrics

We measure outcomes and reliability — not raw message volume. Baselines first; aggressive quotas only after we validate real lead inventory and hours.

Daily

Newly assigned inbound · timely first responses · proactive outreach attempts · meaningful conversations · re-engaged old leads · calls invited · calls booked · callbacks captured · follow-ups completed · CRM records updated.

Weekly

Inbound response-SLA achievement · qualified booked appointments · qualified appointments attended · booking-to-attendance rate · backfill leads reactivated · calls generated from backfill · TPB referrals & verified paid conversions · high-ticket sales attributed to setter-originated opportunities · CRM accuracy · qualified appointments per authorized working hour.

Do not auto-retain the old "100 outbound/day, 20–30 conversations/day, 10 TPB/week, 5 calls/week" as required minimums. Establish a baseline in the first 1–2 weeks against real lead inventory and your approved hours, then set targets that ramp.
EOD report (short & consistent): working hours · new leads handled · responses meeting SLA · meaningful conversations · new bookings · callbacks requested · backfill leads worked · upcoming appointments needing attention · overdue follow-ups · significant escalations · one operational blocker.
