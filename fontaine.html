Dr. Emmanuel Fontaine

DVM, MSc, PhD, DECAR

Chief of Education — Breedwise

Dr. Emmanuel Fontaine holds a Doctor of Veterinary Medicine, a Master of Science in Reproductive Biology, a PhD in Veterinary Genetics, and the Diploma of the European College of Animal Reproduction — designating him among the continent's most rigorously credentialed specialists in canine reproductive medicine. His clinical and academic career has centered on reproductive genetics, breeding program architecture, and the formal translation of veterinary science into applied breeding standards. He is recognized as one of the leading specialists in his field across North America and Europe.

At Breedwise, Dr. Fontaine serves as Chief of Education and the veterinary architect of the Breedwise professional standard. He co-designed the multi-category certification framework that defines professional breeding at a clinical level — specifying the fertility protocols, genetic screening requirements, whelping standards, and neonatal care benchmarks that constitute a Breedwise-compliant program. Every Breedwise Standard Review is assessed against the criteria he established. His benchmark is the standard.

Dr. Fontaine's work bridges the gap between academic veterinary science and the reality of professional breeding practice. He has trained and influenced breeders, veterinary educators, and reproductive specialists across multiple countries. His involvement in Breedwise represents the first time a credential of this clinical caliber has been made systematically available to serious dog breeders outside of accredited veterinary institutions — bringing the rigor of European reproductive medicine directly to the programs that warrant it.
