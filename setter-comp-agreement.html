1. Role & scope

The Setter starts genuine conversations with prospects, qualifies interest, and creates the right next step — a booked Breeder Game Plan Call, a consented callback, or a referral to The Profitable Breeder (TPB) / an educational resource. The Setter does not close Academy or BOSS, run a TPB sales pitch, quote unapproved prices, or provide veterinary advice. Game Plan Calls are run by Regina (qualification) and closed by Austin (Academy/BOSS), or routed to TPB.

2. Base pay

$200.00 per week, paid on the Company's regular schedule, for the agreed coverage hours. The base is not an advance against commission.

3. Commissions & bonuses

ComponentAmountEarned on
TPB origination bonus$20.00Each verified, eligible TPB membership the Setter originates (payment successfully collected).
Academy commission3% of cash collectedAcademy sales on opportunities the Setter sourced & qualified.
BOSS commission3% of cash collectedBOSS sales on opportunities the Setter sourced & qualified.

Commissions are earned on cash actually collected (not contract value). On payment plans, commission is paid as each installment is collected.

4. Attribution (how credit is assigned)

  • Credit follows the documented source & owner recorded in CC360 at first contact — not whoever messaged most recently.
  • The Setter must log and tag each sourced lead to themselves at the start of the conversation. Unlogged work is not credited.
  • Where multiple people touch a lead (e.g., marketing-sourced, Regina on the phone, Austin closing), the Setter earns commission only where they are the documented originating qualifier. Genuine disputes are resolved by the Supervising Manager.
  • Company-assigned or pre-existing leads the Setter did not originate do not carry origination commission unless agreed in writing.

6. Chargebacks, refunds & reversals

Commission is contingent on funds being retained by the Company. If a payment is refunded, charged back, or reversed, the related commission is reversed and deducted from future commission or, if none is available, repaid to the Company.

7. Commission payment timing

Commissions are reconciled from collected funds and paid for the prior period. A statement of credited conversions and collected amounts accompanies each payment.

8. Expectations & conduct

  • Meet the response, booking, CRM-accuracy and reporting standards in the Setter Operating Manual (as updated).
  • Be truthful; make no income or puppy-health guarantees; give no veterinary advice; honor all opt-outs; follow platform rules and approved audiences/tools only.
  • Use only Company-approved accounts and systems; protect account access and confidential information.

9. Confidentiality & Company property

Prospect/member data, pricing, scripts, and systems are confidential and remain the Company's property. On separation, the Setter returns or loses access to all accounts, data, and materials, and does not retain or use Company contacts.

10. Term, at-will & post-termination

This arrangement is and may be ended by either party. After the end date, the Setter is paid commission only on deals booked/originated before separation that collect within of the end date, subject to §6; no commission accrues on activity after separation.
