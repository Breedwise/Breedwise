You already know what it takes to produce a litter. You’ve paid stud fees. Run progesterone. Lost sleep during whelping. Answered endless puppy inquiries. Spent thousands on your dogs. Made placements. Handled unexpected expenses. Learned lessons the expensive way.

Revenue Is Not Profit

Selling the Litter Doesn’t Automatically Mean the Litter Was Profitable.

Money coming in feels like success. But revenue and profit are not the same thing. Every litter carries real costs:

Stud & service feesProgesterone & reproductive careTravel & shippingHealth testingVeterinary careC-section & emergency careWhelping suppliesFoodRegistrationMarketingYour time & laborPuppy careUnexpected complications

If your pricing is primarily based on what other breeders charge, you may have no idea whether that price actually supports the standards you’re trying to maintain.

And profitability isn’t only a pricing problem. If puppies arrive before qualified demand exists, your leverage changes. Now you’re posting harder, answering more messages, entertaining lower-quality inquiries, hearing “what’s your lowest?”, watching puppies get older, and wondering whether you should discount.

A pricing mistake can affect every puppy in the litter. A demand problem becomes obvious when puppies are already on the ground. A preparation gap often becomes visible when the pressure is highest.

Another Litter Is an Expensive Place to Keep Guessing.

Questions, Answered

Who is this for?

Established breeders already producing litters, generally around 2–3+ per year, who want stronger economics, pricing, demand, buyer systems and veterinary-backed preparation. This is not designed as a first-litter course.

I’ve been breeding for years. Will this be too basic?

No. Breeders with 7–13 years of experience have reported meaningful changes after working with BreedWise. The Intensive assumes breeding experience and focuses on the business and preparation systems many experienced breeders never formally built.

Can this actually help me make more money?

We do not guarantee financial outcomes. The Intensive helps you understand and improve the systems that influence business performance, including economics, pricing, positioning, demand, qualification and sales. Your results depend on your program, breed, market, pricing and execution.

Why does veterinary education belong in a profitability event?

Because profitability without standards is not the BreedWise philosophy. A professional breeding program needs both sustainable economics and systems designed around responsible care. That is why Austin and Dr. Fontaine teach the Intensive together.

Is Dr. Fontaine replacing my veterinarian?

Absolutely not. His teaching is educational and designed to help you prepare more effectively and collaborate more effectively with your own veterinarian.

What happens if I miss part of the live event?

General Admission includes 14-day replay access. VIP includes lifetime access to the recordings. Dogs do not care about your calendar, so we built in a cushion.

What’s the difference between General Admission and VIP?

General Admission gives you the system, frameworks, tools, working sessions and completed plan. VIP adds more personalized implementation support and individual expert review of your program.

What do I actually leave with?

You leave with your completed Profitable Breeder Plan™, built around your own Working Litter. It brings together your litter economics, reproductive planning considerations, pricing and value stack, whelping and neonatal readiness, 90-day demand plan, buyer process, 12-month priorities and next 30-day actions.

It is not a collection of disconnected worksheets. It is one connected operating plan for the breeding program you’re actually running.
