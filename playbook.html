Operating System · Austin's Playbook

Run the company by exception,
not by explanation.

This is your day-to-day operating manual. The job is to stop being BreedWise's search engine: decide what only you can decide, put the answer into the system once, and let each role operate and improve their part.

The one distinction that removes most of the ambiguity: Austin decides what the product is. Jennifer makes sure members successfully move through it. When a question is "what should the product / price / policy / science be?" → that's you. When it's "how do we move this member forward?" → that's the role, operating the system.

The bridge that makes the quarter happen: two blocks a day are aimed at your quarterly rocks — the 5:30 AM frog and the 1–4 PM deep work. If those two blocks each move one Quarterly Priority forward, the quarter takes care of itself. Everything else (meetings, outbounds, closing) runs the machine day to day.

How each block is used

Morning
  • Open the Command Center and ask one question: "What's red?" — goal pace, at-risk members, stale leads, outstanding escalations.
  • Scan the Issues / Decisions queue. Make the calls only you can make: pricing, product, policy, scientific-scope, escalated member situations.
  • Anything you can't decide yet gets flagged DECISION REQUIRED with a date — never left vague.
Build blocks
  • Product / Curriculum block — you own what the product is. Move one roadmap item forward.
  • Certification block — daily until Nov 28, 2026. Treat it as a project, not background curriculum work.
All day

When someone asks a recurring question, run this loop instead of answering from memory:

1
Is the answer already in a Role Scorecard, the Current Offer Reference, or an SOP? → point them to it.
2
Answer doesn't exist? → make the decision once.
3
Immediately put it into the system so the same question never reaches you again.
End of day
  • Clear the Issues / Decisions queue to zero-or-dated.
  • Every decision made today that changes an answer → the owner updates the relevant tab before you're asked twice.
Your daily win: you made decisions, not documents — and every recurring question got answered by the system, not by you.

SOP Standard & Index

the SOP is the path
Jennifer drafts the HOW. You approve anything affecting company policy. Tell her: "I've defined your outcomes, ownership, product info and escalation boundaries. Document the repeatable workflows you own. Bring me decisions or gaps that need leadership — not questions the system already answers."

One template for every SOP

  • Name · Owner · Purpose · Trigger · Frequency · SLA
  • Systems Used · Resources Used
  • Handoff · Escalation Owner
  • Procedure — Step 1…5
  • Exceptions — If X → do Y. If Y → escalate to Z.
  • Definition of Done
  • Related Resources (links) · KPI affected · Last Updated · Owner

This fixes the "tools are segmented" complaint: the SOP is the path, the resources are what you use along it.

Escalation Rule & Product Feedback Loop

stop being the search engine
Team rule: Before escalating a recurring operational question, check the Role Scorecard, Current Offer Reference, and relevant SOP. If the answer exists, follow it. If it doesn't, escalate the missing decision — and once resolved, update the system so the same question never needs leadership again.

The product feedback loop

1
Member question → Can Jennifer answer from an approved resource? Yes → answer.
2
No → does the approved answer exist elsewhere? Yes → locate it, update the SOP / library.
3
Still no → isolated or recurring? Isolated → resolve. Recurring → PRODUCT GAP.
4
Route the gap: Business → Austin · Scientific → Emmanuel · Operational → Ops.
5
Product decision → create / update resource → Jennifer closes the member loop → SOP + library updated.

This is what stops you from answering the same question 25 times.
