Is BreedWise veterinary care?

No. BreedWise provides veterinary education, not veterinary diagnosis or treatment. Our goal is to help breeders become more informed and prepared, understand what they should be monitoring, recognize when something may warrant veterinary attention, maintain better records, and communicate more effectively with their veterinary team.

Individual medical decisions should be made with the veterinarian responsible for the animal's care.
