Before you start

How is this different from a course?

A course hands you information and hopes you apply it. B.O.S.S. builds the systems with you and keeps you moving until they're running. You leave with a website, a CRM and operating systems — not a to-do list.

How does the guarantee work?

Do the work, and if your waitlist doesn't collect at least what you paid inside your 120 days, you get every dollar back. Every deposit and sale from your start date counts, tracked on your dashboard toward the exact amount you paid.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. Our team configures the core systems with you and keeps the build moving. You bring your program and your effort; we bring the build and the structure.

What happens after 120 days?

The high-touch build phase ends on purpose, so it never becomes unlimited access. Optional continuation support is available if you want ongoing help keeping your systems operating and improving.
