01

The Ascension Logic

TPB gives a breeder clarity. Academy gives them implementation. The Scorecard reveals the gap — Academy is how they close it. Ascension isn't an upsell; it's the obvious next step once a breeder can finally see what's holding their program back.

Every breeder has two engines — a Breeding Engine (preparation → reproduction → whelping → neonatal → healthy puppies) and a Business Engine (positioning → demand → qualified buyers → sales → margin → reinvestment). TPB helps them diagnose both. Academy helps them rebuild both, with coaching and our veterinary team, over 120 days.

So our job in TPB is not to pitch Academy. It's to deliver real clarity fast, surface the gap, and — for the breeders who are ready and able — extend a genuine invitation to go deeper. If TPB doesn't deliver value on its own, ascension collapses. Serve first, always.

The line we never cross: no member should feel like TPB is a bait-and-switch. TPB is a complete, valuable $97 product. Academy is for the breeders who finish TPB's diagnosis and decide they want help implementing.

The 3 Questions Every New Member Answers

Set these as the membership questions in Skool (Settings → Membership → Questions). When a member joins, our Skool webhook automatically logs their answers to their CC360 contact as a note — so the Setter and Director see them without asking again. Three questions, no more: enough to route them, not enough to create friction.

Question 1 · Where they are
Where is your breeding program right now?
Options: Planning my first litter · 1–3 litters in · Established (4+ litters) · Established, but stuck / plateaued
Why: Stage decides the ascension path. "Established" and "stuck/plateaued" are your strongest Academy candidates — they have a program worth investing in and a reason to. "Planning first litter" usually needs TPB time first.
Question 2 · What they want fixed
If we could help you fix ONE thing in your program in the next 90 days, what would it be?
Options: Demand & waitlist · Sales & pricing · Profitability / knowing my numbers · Whelping & keeping puppies alive · Health testing & breeding decisions · Systems & consistency
Why: This is the wedge. It names the exact pain the Setter opens with later, and it tells them which engine (business vs breeding) the member cares about most. It also routes which content and community channels to point them to first.
Question 3 · Their ambition (the ascension signal)
What would need to change in your program for this year to be a breakthrough year?
Format: Open text.
Why: Ambition is the single best ascension predictor. A big, specific answer ("I want to go from 2 litters at $2K to a real waitlist at $5K") = an Academy candidate. A small or vague answer = serve them in TPB and re-check at Day 30. The Setter quotes this answer back to them in the invite.
What you need to do: add these three to Skool membership questions. Nothing else — the webhook already captures the answers into CC360. If you want them to also drive automatic tags, tell me the exact option values and I'll map Question 1 & 2 to CC360 tags (e.g. stage-established wants-pricing) so the Setter can filter ready members instantly.
04

Community Triggers That Prompt Ascension

These are the moments inside Skool that surface or nudge a member toward Academy. Some are automated; most are the Director watching the community and acting with intent. Auto = the system fires it. Human = someone must do it. Skool setup = configure once in Skool.

Auto The Scorecard is the #1 trigger

Completing the Program Scorecard tags the member in CC360 with a readiness verdict — academy-ready (42–77/100, coachable middle with real gaps), academy-watch (78+, already strong), or academy-nurture (<42, foundation first) — plus a note with their scores, priority, and focus. Re-scoring with a 6+ point jump adds tpb-improving. This is live now. The Scorecard is pinned as "Start Here."

Human The "Win" post

When a member posts a win (sold a litter, filled a waitlist, first clean whelp) → Director reacts, celebrates publicly, then DMs: "This is exactly the kind of momentum Academy is built to accelerate — want me to show you what that looks like?" A win is the warmest possible ascension moment.

Human The "Stuck" post

When a member posts an implementation problem (not just an info question) → help them genuinely in the thread, then flag: "This is a great TPB question — and it's exactly the kind of thing we build out with you inside Academy." Serve first, plant the seed second.

Skool setup A milestone level that unlocks an Academy invite

Configure a Skool level (e.g. Level 3) reached through participation. Hitting it means the member is engaged and getting value — unlock a "Ready for the next step?" post at that level. Engagement earns the invitation.

Human Live call / office-hours attendance

Showing up live = high intent. Note attendees; the Director or Setter follows up with the members whose questions were implementation-heavy.

Auto + Human The Day-30 re-scorecard

Prompt every member to re-take the Scorecard at Day 30. Improvement (tpb-improving) proves they execute — the best sign they'll thrive in Academy. Stalled scores = they need help implementing, which is Academy. Either way, it's an ascension conversation.

The CC360 Sequence (Automated)

These messages are timed on wait triggers in CC360 and run in the background. Human touches (below) should complement them, not collide — check the member's timeline before you reach out so you're not landing on top of an automated send. Suggested ascension-specific copy to plug into the sequence:

Day 0 · Welcome (email + SMS) — sent on joinWelcome to The Profitable Breeder. Your very first step is your Program Scorecard — it takes ~10 minutes and shows you exactly where your program is strong and where the gaps are. Start here 👉 [scorecard link]
Day 2 · Scorecard nudge (SMS) — if not completedHey [first name] — did you get a chance to run your Scorecard yet? It's the fastest way to know what deserves your attention first. Takes 10 min: [link]
Day 30 · Ascension seed (email) — tag: academy-readyYour Scorecard flagged [priority area] as your biggest opportunity. That's exactly the kind of thing we help breeders rebuild inside Academy — with coaching and our vet team, over 120 days. If you want to see whether it's a fit, grab a Fit Call: [book link]
Day 60 / 90 · Program review (email) — tag: academy-ready or tpb-improvingYou've had [X] days in TPB. Let's look at what's changed and what's next. If you're ready to stop diagnosing and start implementing, book a Fit Call and we'll map your next 12 months: [book link]
Rule of thumb: automation opens the door and keeps it warm; the human DM is what actually books the call. The sequence should never be the only thing inviting them — it's the backstop for members the Setter hasn't reached yet.

Setter Playbook & Scripts

When to reach out (any one of these):

  • Member gets tagged academy-ready (Scorecard) — check CC360 daily
  • Big, specific answer on Join Question 3 (ambition)
  • Posted a win, or a "stuck" implementation problem
  • Re-scored with a 6+ point jump (tpb-improving)
  • Attended a live call and asked an implementation question

The rule: never open with Academy. Open with them — their scorecard, their win, their answer. Earn the conversation, then invite.

Opening DM — off the ScorecardHey [first name] — I saw you ran your Scorecard 👏 Your program's clearly [genuine strength, e.g. "further along than most on breeding prep"]. The one thing that stood out as your biggest lever right now is [priority area]. Mind if I ask — is that the piece you're most wanting to fix this year?

(Wait for a reply. You're diagnosing, not pitching.)
Opening DM — off a win[first name] this is huge 🔥 [specific win]. Genuinely happy for you. Quick q — is this the direction you want to keep building, or are you trying to make it repeatable so it's not a one-off? (A "make it repeatable" answer is your Academy opening.)
The invite — after they've named the gapThat's exactly the kind of thing we go deep on inside Academy — it's 120 days where we actually help you build [their gap] into your program, with coaching and our vet team, not just more videos. Want to hop on a short Fit Call to see if it's right for where you are? No pressure — if it's not a fit, I'll tell you. Here's my calendar: [book.html link]

Objection handling:

They sayYou say
"I just joined TPB.""Perfect — TPB is exactly where you should be right now. The Fit Call is just to see if and when Academy makes sense. No rush at all."
"What does it cost?""It's a $6,000 investment, and there are payment options. But the call is about fit first — let's make sure it's even right for your program before we talk numbers."
"I'm not sure I have time.""That's the exact reason it works — it's done with you, so you're not figuring it out alone. The Fit Call is 20 minutes. Worst case you leave with a clearer plan."

The brief to Austin (log in the CC360 note before the call)

  • Scorecard: overall + priority area + focus
  • Their Join Q3 answer (their ambition, in their words)
  • The gap they told you they most want fixed
  • Any win or context from the community
  • Stage: how many litters, current pricing if known

Director of Member Success Playbook

Daily

  • Scan the community — react to and celebrate every win, answer every stuck post
  • Plant one ascension seed where it's genuinely warranted (never force it)
  • Welcome new members and point them to the Scorecard ("Start Here")

Weekly

  • Pull the CC360 list of academy-ready members from the past 7 days → hand to the Setter with one line of context each
  • Check Scorecard completion rate — chase anyone past Day 7 who hasn't done it
  • Flag tpb-improving members to the Setter — they're the hottest

Always

  • Protect the experience. TPB must feel like a complete win at $97. Over-pitching Academy kills retention and referrals.
  • Activation is the job. A member who completes the Scorecard and posts once is 10× more likely to ascend. Get them active in week one.
