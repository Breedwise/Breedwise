Questions

Good to know

How do I reserve a puppy?

Start with an application. Once we've connected and confirmed the right match, a deposit reserves your puppy. We'll keep you updated with photos and milestones until go-home day.

Do you health test and guarantee your puppies?

Yes. Health is our first priority. Our dogs are health tested, and every puppy goes home with a written health guarantee, a vet check, and age-appropriate vaccinations.

Can you deliver my puppy?

Yes — we offer safe delivery options nationwide, as well as in-person pick-up. We'll help you choose the best option for your family.

When can my puppy come home?

Puppies go home once they're fully weaned, vet-cleared, and developmentally ready — never before it's healthy for them. We'll give you an exact date as it approaches.

What if I'm not ready yet?

That's completely okay. Join our waitlist and we'll reach out when a puppy that fits your family becomes available. No pressure, ever.
