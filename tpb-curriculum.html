Build rules

Guardrails for every module

  • One lever, not the masterclass. Teach the 80/20 move for the week. Enough for a win; the full depth and the build are the Academy. This is how we preserve ascension.
  • Every module ends the same way: the weekly win (a tangible output) → the assessment score it moves → the ceiling (what fully built looks like, with us).
  • State the map out loud. Every module opens with "This is [Area] — one of the ten from your assessment," so the breeder always knows where they are.
  • Breeding modules measure preparedness and systems — never promises of pregnancy rates, litter size, survival or medical outcomes. That stays with their vet.
  • The pull to Academy is structural, not salesy. DIY vs. done-with-you does the selling. We never have to hard-pitch.
