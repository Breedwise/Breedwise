Built for serious breeders

You're committed to your breed.
Is your business built to support it?

You study pedigrees. You invest in health testing and veterinary care. You make thoughtful breeding decisions.

But none of that automatically guarantees qualified puppy buyers, a consistent waitlist, or a financially sustainable program.

Great breeders deserve profitable businesses.

Whether you're preserving an established breed, improving your current program, or preparing for your first litter, you'll learn how to build a qualified puppy waitlist, communicate the value of responsible breeding, and create a business that supports your standards.

You shouldn't have to sacrifice your breeding standards to build a profitable business. BreedWise teaches you how to pursue both.

What you'll learn

In this training, discover

  • ✓How to build a qualified puppy waitlist before the litter arrives, so homes are ready when the puppies are.
  • ✓Why followers and puppy inquiries don't automatically become committed buyers, and what actually does.
  • ✓3 reproductive, whelping and neonatal risks to prepare for with your veterinary team before your next litter.
  • ✓How to communicate your program's standards and value to the right families.
  • ✓How to evaluate litter costs and real profitability, without cutting corners on your dogs.

The Profitable Litter Readiness Scorecard

A brief assessment so you know exactly where your biggest gap is, and leave with useful guidance whether or not you take it further.

Breeding readiness

Have you reviewed applicable health screenings, reproduction planning and whelping preparation with appropriate veterinary support?

Buyer readiness

Do you have identifiable, appropriately qualified prospective puppy buyers, and a process for following up?

Business readiness

Do you understand your expected costs, required reserves and potential financial risks?
