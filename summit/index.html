When and where is it?
The Summit runs Thursday evening, October 1 through Sunday, October 4, 2026, in person in Dallas, Texas. The exact venue is shared once you're registered so we can keep the room small and focused.

Yes. This is an implementation room for breeders who are already operating (or imminently whelping) and want to build a premium, ethical, six-figure program.

We cap the room at 20 breeders so our vets and team can give everyone real, hands-on attention, including 1:1 coaching and live audits. Applying makes sure the room stays serious and the right fit for you.
