The real math

Your Breeding Program Has More Than One Kind of ROI

You can lose profitability because puppies don't sell. But you can also lose profitability because…

  • Your pricing doesn't reflect your true costs
  • You don't have enough qualified buyers
  • Buyers inquire but don't convert
  • You don't know what your litters actually make
  • Reproductive setbacks consume time and money
  • Preventable problems become expensive problems
  • Concerning changes aren't recognized early enough
  • Poor records make patterns harder to identify
  • Your program depends on you remembering everything

A stronger breeding program improves both sides of that equation.
