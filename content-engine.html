The Content Engine

One content machine → multiple platforms. The goal is not to create completely different content for every platform. Create once. Adapt. Distribute everywhere.

PlatformShort-formStoriesCarousel / Long
Instagram5 Reels/day5/day1 carousel/day
Facebook5 Reels/day5/day1 carousel/day
TikTok5 videos/day5/day where applicable1 carousel/photo/day where applicable
YouTube5 Shorts/day—2 long-form/week
These are primarily the same core assets intelligently distributed, with platform-specific hooks, captions, CTAs, titles, formatting or edits where needed. That turns an impossible volume problem into a distribution system.

Weekly Operating Rhythm

Yasmin's week should be predictable. She shouldn't spend every day reacting to "what do we post today?"

Mon
Analytics + ideation + weekly priorities
Tue
Scripts / frameworks + carousel batch
Wed
Distribution + pipeline + upcoming batch prep
Thu
Batch filming / production as scheduled
Fri
Analytics + winners/losers + next week's optimization
Publishing and community monitoring continue daily. Today's content was decided two weeks ago. Today's performance decides what gets created next.

Content Pipeline

Maintain at least 14 days of ready-to-publish content at all times.

Ideas→Scripted→Ready to film→Filmed→Editing→Approved→Scheduled→Published→Analyzed
A simple Google Sheet is enough initially. Don't overbuild this.

The Weekly Content Meeting

30–45 minutes. Answer five questions, then end with assigned owners and deadlines.

What won?Which content generated the most qualified attention / leads?
Why did it win?Topic? Hook? Person? Format? Emotion? CTA?
What lost?What consistently underperformed?
What do we make more of?Turn winners into series instead of constantly starting over.
What's blocking production?Austin? Emmanuel? Editing? Approvals? Ideas? Distribution?
