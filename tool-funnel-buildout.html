Tool → TPB → BOSS Funnel Buildout

The front-door funnel for cold social traffic: a free tool proves value, a human (the setter) brings them into The Profitable Breeder, then we ascend them to BOSS. This is the CC360 build and the automation layer that makes strangers convert.

The operating rule

Strangers don't buy from an ad. They convert when the tool proves we know their world, a human reaches them fast, and TPB is an easy first yes. Speed-to-lead and the TPB-first ladder are the whole game.

Social post / ad→ Tool opt-in→ Tool completed→ Setter call→ TPB member→ BOSS member

2 · Opt-in email sequence

Fires the moment someone opts into a tool. One skeleton, with the tool name and result swapped in per tool. The sequence exits the instant they join TPB (then the ascension track in §6 takes over). Each email gives value and points to one action: talk to us / join TPB.
Instant
Email 1 — “Your [Tool] results are ready”

Deliver the result/link, one line of value, and set the expectation that a BreedWise coach will reach out to walk through it. Warm, human, from the setter's name.

CTA: Open your results
Day 1
Email 2 — “The 3 moves from your [Tool]”

Interpret their result and give the top 3 moves for the next 90 days. Invite a quick, free call to walk through them with a coach.

CTA: Grab a time / reply
Day 2
Email 3 — one real insight tied to their gap

Teach one concrete thing (a change in the science, a pricing or buyer-process fix). Position The Profitable Breeder as where breeders actually implement this.

CTA: See The Profitable Breeder
Day 4
Email 4 — “Knowing isn't the same as doing”

The gap between a score and a stronger program is implementation. TPB is the first step, with the community and the plan. Soft offer.

CTA: Join The Profitable Breeder
Day 7
Email 5 — last touch

“Want a hand with your next litter?” One more invite to book the call or join TPB, then move to monthly nurture if no action.

CTA: Book a call / Join TPB
Tool-specific swaps. Healthy Litter → Dr. Fontaine voice, the science angle (we already have this 3-email set built). Profitable Breeder Scorecard → business/profit angle. Keep subject lines about their result, not about us. I can produce the full HTML emails per tool (like the Healthy Litter set) on request.

3 · SMS sequence (setter voice)

SMS from the setter's number does the heavy lifting on cold leads. Short, personal, one ask. Send only to opt-ins who gave a phone number and consented.
Instant (~5 min after opt-in)Hi [First], it's Regina with BreedWise. Your [Tool] is ready here: [link]. I'll give you a quick call to walk through your results. What time works today or tomorrow?
Day 1 — if not completedDid you get a chance to finish your [Tool]? Takes about 5 minutes and shows your top 3 moves: [link]
On completionNice, I see you finished. Want me to walk you through your top 3 moves? I've got time [today/tomorrow] — what's better for you?
No answer after callsTried you today. Still happy to go over your results whenever — just reply with a time and I'll call.
Compliance. Only text opt-ins who provided a phone number; include an opt-out (“reply STOP”) on the first message; honor quiet hours. Confirm CC360 SMS is provisioned for the sending number.

5 · Follow-up sequences

No contact
  • 4 call attempts over ~48h + SMS + one voicemail.
  • No connect → Nurture: weekly value email, setter re-touch at [30] days.
Talked, not ready
  • Tag the reason; set a dated re-touch.
  • Nurture emails tied to their tool result until they re-engage.
Booked call, no-show
  • Instant “sorry we missed you” SMS + rebook link.
  • 2 rebook attempts, then Nurture.
Joined TPB → ascension
  • Exit cold sequences; start onboarding.
  • After ~[7–14] active days, trigger the BOSS invite + book the BOSS call.

7 · Backfill the leads who already opted in

Good news: everyone who ever opted into a tool is already a CC360 contact — the tool functions upsert them with tags on opt-in and completion. So the backfill is a set of CC360 Smart Lists, run inside CC360, no import needed.
List A — Completed (warmest)
  • Tag is ANY of: litter-scorecard-complete, science-scorecard-complete, pb-scorecard-complete, tpb-scorecard-complete
  • AND NOT tpb-member / boss-member
  • → bulk-add to pipeline at stage 2 (Tool Completed); assign Regina; start the completed-lead cadence.
List B — Opted in, didn't finish
  • Tag is ANY of: litter-scorecard-optin, science-scorecard-optin, pb-scorecard-optin, tpb-scorecard-started
  • AND NOT the matching …-complete, AND NOT tpb-member
  • → add at stage 1; start the “finish your tool” nurture.
  • De-dupe by email + phone before enrolling; exclude current TPB/BOSS members.
  • Prioritize List A by recency — call the last 30 days first.
On exporting the raw lead list: I can't bulk-export the opt-in contact PII from the database for you — that data-dump path is intentionally restricted. It isn't needed: the contacts already live in CC360 with the tags above, so build the Smart Lists there. If any opt-in failed to reach CC360 (the tool logged it but the CC360 upsert errored), those show in the submissions vault with no CC360 match — flag those and we can reconcile a short list by hand.

9 · What to focus on first

  1. Speed-to-lead. Instant setter notification + the first SMS within 5 minutes of opt-in. This moves conversion more than anything else.
  2. Standardize the tool-lead tag + owner-regina on every opt-in, so one workflow runs the whole funnel (small edit to the opt-in functions).
  3. Build the opt-in email + SMS sequence and the setter call cadence (§2–§4).
  4. Backfill Lists A and B in CC360; call List A, last 30 days first.
  5. Wire the TPB → BOSS ascension trigger so every new TPB member gets the BOSS conversation on schedule.
