Build blueprint

System Spec

KPI formulas, activation steps & permissions — the reference this interface is built from.

KPI formulas — Director

churn% = cancels ÷ active_at_start · target < 3%
activation% = activated ÷ new · target ≥ 80%
NPS = %promoters − %detractors · target > 80
failed_pay% = members_w_failed ÷ active · < 3%

Activation — 6 binary steps

  • Onboarding call ≤48h
  • Strategy w/ Austin ≤7d
  • Vet strategy ≤14d
  • Joined & welcomed ≤7d
  • First community call week 1
  • First masterclass week 1

KPI formulas — Community

week1% = on both wk-1 calls ÷ new · ≥ 80%
ongoing% = ≥1 call in 3-of-4 wks ÷ active · ≥ 70%
engagement = meaningful posts ÷ active ÷ wk · ≥ 1

KPI formulas — Accountability Leader

pod_attend% = pod on ≥1 call ÷ active pod
homework% = items done ÷ assigned
pod_churn% = pod cancels ÷ pod active

Ramp (d1–60): 2/3→$100, 3/3→$200. Ongoing (d61+): 3/3→$200, else $0.
