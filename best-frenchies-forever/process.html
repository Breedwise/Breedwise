The Process

Simple, honest, and built around the puppy

We keep things easy and transparent from the first message to the day your Frenchie comes home.

Apply

Fill out a quick application so we can learn about your home and what you're looking for.

Talk with Russell

We'll connect by call or text, answer your questions, and help you choose the right puppy.

Reserve

A deposit holds your puppy. We keep you updated with photos and milestones as they grow.

Go home

Pick up in person or we'll arrange safe delivery. Your puppy leaves fully prepared for the transition.

Included With Every Puppy

Your puppy package

Every Best Frenchies Forever puppy goes home ready, with everything you need to start on the right paw.

  • 6-panel DNA & OFA health testing on the program behind your puppy.
  • A complete puppy package to ease the first days at home.
  • Potty training and basic training started before go-home day.
  • Age-appropriate vet care and a clean bill of health.
  • A lifetime health guarantee and lifetime support, directly from Russell.
