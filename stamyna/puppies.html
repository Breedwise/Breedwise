How placement works

Apply first. Then we talk.

Rather than first-come checkout, we place our puppies with families who are prepared for a working guardian breed. You apply, we talk through your home and your goals, and together we decide whether a Stamyna Boerboel is the right fit, for you and for the dog.

A deposit reserves your puppy and is applied to the balance. Every puppy goes home with its health records, vaccinations, microchip, and our go-home support.
