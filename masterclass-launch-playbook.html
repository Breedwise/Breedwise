Step 1

Go-live checklist

The funnel is built and serving. Below is what is already done, then the short list that is yours to flip it fully live. Nothing here needs new tools, just connecting what exists.

Your turn (to flip it fully live)
  • 1Pick the public URL. review.breedwise.io is set to noindex (fine for warm traffic and the team). For paid ads / public launch, either remove noindex on these three pages or point a public funnel domain at them. Decide this first, everything else hangs off the live URL.
  • 2Build one CC360 workflow off the profitable-litter-blueprint tag: send the confirmation email (Section 3A), then the reminder sequence (3B) and SMS (Section 4). This is the only automation you have to build for launch.
  • 3Connect the calendar. In the Typeform qualifier, confirm the "Yes / Maybe" outcome redirects to Regina's calendar and the "Just exploring" outcome to Skool. Set the calendar's post-booking redirect to application-received.html?booked=1.
  • 4Verify Typeform prefill. The scorecard now passes hidden fields on the query string (?first_name=&last_name=&email=&phone=&utm_*). Confirm your Typeform has hidden fields with those exact names so the qualifier arrives prefilled.

  • 6If running paid ads: add the Meta Pixel + CAPI Lead event on the registration page (dedupe browser + server) and a Schedule event on the booked page. Not needed for organic / list launch.
Minimum to go live today: items 1, 2 and 3. Reminders, SMS and ads make it better, but a working page + confirmation email + connected calendar is a live funnel.
Step 2

One pipeline for every lead magnet

Do not build a pipeline per lead magnet. Build one pipeline. Every magnet (this scorecard, the Profitable Breeder Scorecard, Healthy Litter, the Puppy Content Playbook) drops a lead into the same five stages and is told apart by a source tag. One pipeline, one nurture, clean reporting.

01

New Opt-In

Any lead magnet form submitted. Auto-tagged by source.

02

Magnet Engaged

Completed the scorecard / watched the training. Higher intent.

03

Call Booked

Picked a time on Regina's calendar.

04

Showed / Qualified

Attended the call and fits a program.

05

Won / Nurture

Enrolled (Academy / Accelerator), or moved to long-term nurture.

source: profitable-litter source: profitable-breeder source: healthy-litter source: puppy-content
The rule: a lead's stage tells you how close they are to a call. Their source tag tells you which magnet brought them in and which first email to send. Move people by stage, segment reports by source. That is the whole system.

Step 6

Launching every other lead magnet

Same system, every time. When you spin up a new lead magnet, do not reinvent, clone this.

Build the capture page and post it to the opt-in function with a new source tag (e.g. source: healthy-litter).
Drop it into the one pipeline at stage 1 (New Opt-In). No new pipeline.
Write one confirmation email that delivers the magnet, then let it join the standing weekly newsletter.
If it has a live event, clone the reminder sequence (Section 3B) and the SMS (Section 4) and swap the date and links.
Report by source tag. Same five stages, so you can compare magnets on one board.
That is the whole playbook. One pipeline, source tags, one nurture, a reminder sequence per event. Resist the urge to build a separate system for each magnet, that is what makes funnels rot.
