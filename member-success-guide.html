02

The Escalation Rule

LIVE
Before you escalate a recurring question, check three places: your Role Scorecard, the Current Offer Reference, and the relevant SOP. If the answer exists, follow it. If it doesn't, escalate the missing decision — and once it's resolved, it gets written into the system so no one has to ask again.

What to do with any member question

1
Can you answer from an approved resource? Yes → answer.
2
No → does the approved answer exist elsewhere? Yes → locate it, then update the SOP / library so it's findable.
3
Still no → is it isolated or recurring? Isolated → resolve it. Recurring → it's a Product Gap.
4
Route the gap: Business → Austin · Scientific → Emmanuel · Operational → Ops.
5
Decision comes back → resource created/updated → you close the member loop → SOP + library updated.

Weekly Member Success Meeting

LIVE

30 minutes · come prepared

  • 5 min — Scoreboard. What's red?
  • 10 min — Exceptions only: at-risk members, stuck members, Academy opportunities, outstanding expert escalations, certification issues, major wins.
  • 10 min — Issues needing Austin: pricing exception, product decision, curriculum gap, policy decision, escalated member situation.
  • 5 min — Commitments: who / what / by when.

We don't walk member-by-member through people who are fine. We manage the exceptions.
