Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

How does the process work?+

We start by learning your program, your breed, and your goals. From there our team films and builds your content, builds your website, maps your breeding and genetics strategy, and designs a sales process you run. You stay the breeder, we build the business around you.

What exactly does BOSS build for my program?+

Your content engine, your website, your sales process, and your breeding and genetics strategy, delivered by one team. The goal is a program that runs like a real business and sells your own puppies at premium prices, not a course you have to go implement alone.

Do I need a big following or fancy equipment already?+

No. You film simple clips we direct you through on your phone; we handle the rest. The system is built to create authority and demand from where you are now.

I've tried the business side before and it didn't stick. Why is this different?+

Because it's done with you, not handed to you. Most breeders stall because the marketing and sales work is a second full-time job. We take that off your plate and build it properly, grounded in real breeding standards and veterinary science, not hype.

How much is it, and is there a guarantee?+

BOSS is a premium, capped program and pricing is covered on your call so it can be matched to your program. It's backed by our guarantee: your puppies earn it back, or your money back.

Who is this not for?+

It's not for anyone looking to cut corners on health or ethics, and it's not for brand-new breeders without dogs or litters yet. BOSS is for established breeders ready to build a real, respected, profitable program.
