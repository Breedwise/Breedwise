Questions
Before you apply

How is this different from a course?

A course hands you information and hopes you apply it. B.O.S.S. builds the systems with you and keeps you moving until they're running. You leave with a website, a CRM and operating systems — not a to-do list.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. Our team configures the core systems with you and keeps the build moving. You bring your program and your effort; we bring the build and the structure.

What exactly gets built?

Your baseline and 120-day pathway, your website, your CRM and lead system, and the waitlist, workflows, dashboards, templates and trackers your program needs — across both the breeding and business sides.

How much of my time does it take?

It's a 120-day commitment with formal reviews at Day 30, 60, 90 and 120, strategy sessions, and a private channel so you can get unstuck fast. The more you show up, the more gets built.
