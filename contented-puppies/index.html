Health & How We Raise

The care behind every puppy

Every puppy is raised underfoot in our home, surrounded by family, handled daily, and thoughtfully socialized, never in a kennel. Our dogs are in the process of genetic and OFA health testing, and each family receives their puppy's health records at go-home.

We believe beautiful type, gentle temperament, and real health all begin with how a puppy spends its very first weeks.
