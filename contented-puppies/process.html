The best welcome is in person. When distance makes that hard, a trusted pet nanny flies with your puppy and meets you at the airport.

Pick Up in Person

ii.

Pet-Nanny Delivery

A pet nanny flies with your puppy and delivers them to you at the agreed airport, keeping you informed the whole way. Typically $500 from the East Coast through the Midwest, and $600–$700 to the West Coast. We confirm the exact fee before any travel is booked.

Every Puppy Goes Home

Healthy, and ready for life with you

Every puppy is vet-checked, receives a series of puppy vaccinations and deworming, and is sold with a written one-year health guarantee. Your puppy's current records go home with them.

We also ask every family to feed Life's Abundance for the first year, which keeps the health guarantee valid and gives your puppy the best start.

Questions & Answers

Common questions

If your question is not here, we are always glad to answer it personally.

How do I reserve a puppy?

Choose your puppy and talk with us by phone or live video. When it is the right fit, reserve them with the deposit shown on their profile. We then send the health guarantee and records, you sign the agreement, and we confirm the balance and homecoming plan.

How much is the deposit?

Most puppies reserve with a $500 deposit, which is applied toward the full price. Each puppy's profile shows their full price and exact deposit before you commit to anything.

Do you deliver, or is it pickup only?

Both. You are welcome to pick your puppy up in person. For families farther away, a pet nanny flies with your puppy and meets you at the airport, typically $500 from the East Coast through the Midwest and $600–$700 to the West Coast. We confirm the exact fee before booking travel.

Can I meet the puppy before I reserve?

Absolutely. We encourage a live video visit so you can see your puppy and ask every question before making any payment.

What health documentation comes with my puppy?

Every puppy is vet-checked, vaccinated, and dewormed, and goes home with their current records and a written one-year health guarantee. Read the full guarantee.

What food do you recommend?

We feed and recommend Life's Abundance. Feeding it for the first year is also required to keep the health guarantee valid.

Do you keep a waitlist?

Yes. If today's puppies are not quite the right fit, join our waitlist and you will be among the first to know when new litters arrive.

¿Hablan español?

Sí. Con gusto le ayudamos en español, escríbanos o llámenos cuando guste.

Have a question, or ready to begin?

We would love to hear from you and help you find your perfect companion.
