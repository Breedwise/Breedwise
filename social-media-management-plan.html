The DM-to-call engine

This is where a "social media account" becomes a booking machine. Posting creates the audience; the DM engine converts it. Yasmin runs this every business day.

2:00–4:00 PM

Your daily call block. Every day, Yasmin's goal is to fill this two-hour window with qualified conversations. Booking into one consistent block keeps your calendar predictable, your show-up rate high, and your day protected. Keep it open — it's the whole point.

The five-step DM framework (no spray-and-pray)

We never open with a pitch. We open with a person. This is the best-practice social-selling sequence Yasmin follows on every conversation.

Step 1

Connect

Genuine, personalized opener off something real — their comment, story, or profile. Zero copy-paste.

Step 2

Qualify

A natural question that surfaces their situation and whether they're a fit — before any offer is mentioned.

Step 3

Add Value

Give first — a tip, resource, or insight. Earn the right to ask for the call by being useful.

Step 4

Invite

Soft, confident invitation to the 2–4 PM call. Framed as the logical next step, not a hard sell.

Step 5

Confirm

Lock the time, send a reminder, reduce no-shows. A booked call isn't real until it's confirmed.

Speed-to-lead

Inbound DMs are answered fast — target within 1 business hour during active hours. The faster the reply, the higher the booking rate. Leads go cold in hours, not days.

Warm before cold

Priority order: people who engaged, viewed stories, saved, or messaged first. These are pre-warmed and convert far better than cold outreach.

Multi-touch follow-up

No one is dropped after one message. A structured, respectful follow-up cadence recovers the majority of bookings — most sales happen after the first "not yet."
