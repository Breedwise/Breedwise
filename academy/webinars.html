Built for serious breeders

You're committed to your breed.
Is your business built to support it?

You study pedigrees. You invest in health testing and veterinary care. You make thoughtful breeding decisions. But none of that automatically guarantees qualified buyers, a consistent waitlist, or a financially sustainable program.

Great breeders deserve profitable businesses, without sacrificing their standards.

In this training

What you'll walk away with.

  • How to build a qualified puppy waitlist before the litter arrives, so homes are ready when the puppies are.
  • Why followers and puppy inquiries don't automatically become committed buyers, and what actually does.
  • Three reproductive, whelping and neonatal risks to prepare for with your veterinary team before your next litter.
  • How to communicate your program's standards and value to the right families.
  • How to evaluate litter costs and real profitability, without cutting corners on your dogs.
The framework

The three systems behind a better litter business.

Producing a litter responsibly and finding the right homes aren't separate challenges. They're the same business.

Profit

Litter economics, sustainable pricing and the business systems that keep what you earn.
