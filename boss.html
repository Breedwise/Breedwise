BOSS™ Implementation

120 Days · $6,000 · paid in full or 4 × $1,500
Build
01

BOSS™ Baseline Assessment

Know where you stand before deciding what to build.
Establish the member's starting point across both sides of the program:
  • Breeding program & business
  • Existing systems & current challenges
  • Relevant KPIs
  • Upcoming breeding / litter timing
  • 120-day goals
  • Highest-priority gaps
OutputA documented BOSS Baseline.
02

Personalized 120-Day Implementation Pathway

Know exactly what you're building.
The baseline becomes a customized implementation pathway. Each member receives:
Priority→System→Training→Tool→Implementation→KPI→Definition of Done
The member can always answer: What am I working on right now, why does it matter, and what does "done" look like?
03

BreedWise Website

Build the digital foundation.
A professional website built around the breeder's program, positioning, credibility and buyer journey.
SOP defines scope separately — pages, copy responsibilities, assets required, revision limits, domain/hosting, integrations, timeline, what BreedWise builds vs. what the breeder provides, and what happens after Day 120 — so "website included" never becomes unlimited agency work.
04

CRM + Lead Management System

Organize the buyer journey.
Infrastructure to manage leads, inquiries, qualified buyers, follow-up, pipeline stages, buyer communication, customer records, relevant automations and sales activity.
The valueYour lead-management system configured and operating — not simply CRM access.
05

BOSS™ System Configuration

Don't just hand them software. Help install the system.
BreedWise helps configure the core systems the pathway requires: website, CRM, workflows, dashboards, tracking, templates and other applicable BreedWise systems.
The strongest distinction from TPB: TPB teaches and diagnoses. BOSS implements and reviews.

12

BOSS™ Implementation Reviews

Day 30 · Day 60 · Day 90 · Day 120.
Formal checkpoints prevent the pathway from becoming "four months of access." Every review answers: What was supposed to happen? What was completed? What's working? What's blocked? What changed? What's next?
Every review endsPriority → Next Action → Owner → Deadline.
Measure
13

BOSS™ Progress Dashboard + KPI Tracking

Make progress visible.
  • Implementation — Not Started → In Progress → Implemented → Optimizing
  • Business — demand, inquiry, buyer, conversion, pricing, economics and operational metrics
  • Breeding Program — preparedness, recordkeeping, monitoring consistency, process completion and other appropriate non-medical indicators
  • Pathway — 30 / 60 / 90 / 120-day progress
Breeding indicators measure preparedness and implementation — never promises of pregnancy rates, litter size, puppy survival or medical outcomes. The dashboard answers: are we actually implementing what we said we would?

Prove — The Certification Pathway
The BOSS™ Certification Pathway
120 Days to Build. 12 Months to Prove.
The initial 120 days count toward the member's qualifying participation period. After at least 12 months of qualifying BOSS participation, the member can become eligible for formal certification review. 12 months makes you eligible. It does not make you certified.
Earned through: Knowledge + Verification + Implementation + Audit
Gate 1
Knowledge
Demonstrate competency across defined BreedWise education and standards.
Gate 2
Verification
Provide documentation and evidence around program practices, records, health/genetics organization, preparedness and standards.
Gate 3
Implementation
Show required BOSS systems and practices are actually being used — not merely understood.
Gate 4
Audit
BreedWise reviews the submitted evidence against the defined certification standard.
CertifiedConditional — corrections requiredNot yet certified
Certification is standards-based, not subscription-based.
27

BreedWise Certified™ Status

A credential that compounds.
Credential→Certification mark→Consumer trust→Directory / profile→Verification→Renewal
Certification verifies compliance with the defined BreedWise standard. It never implies BreedWise guarantees pregnancy rates, litter sizes, puppy survival, absence of disease, profitability, or the medical appropriateness of an individual breeding.
28

Certification Maintenance & Renewal

Membership and certification are related — but not the same product.
Certification has its own term and maintenance requirements (e.g. a 12-month certification term with defined maintenance/renewal). Canceling BOSS continuation does not automatically erase an already-earned certification. That protects credibility.
