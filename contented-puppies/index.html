I — Our Story

Raised with love,
purpose & intention

What began in 2005 as a love for this beautiful breed has grown into a lifelong passion, and a commitment to the qualities we treasure most.

Over the years, my husband and I have worked diligently to raise puppies who are not only beautiful, with gorgeous heavy coats and the shorter, cobby structure we love, but most importantly, who are healthy, well-balanced, confident, and wonderfully socialized.

Eight years ago, another family joined us in this journey, and our shared love for the breed has made it even more special. Together we raise every puppy in a warm, family-centered home, surrounded by people, handled with care, and given the individual attention they need to thrive.

Every puppy carries with them a little piece of our hearts, and it is our hope they bring your family the same love, joy, and companionship this breed has brought into ours.

II — The Standard

Twenty years, one unwavering standard

With more than two decades behind us, we stand apart through what we refuse to compromise.

i.

Health First

Our dogs are in the process of genetic and OFA health testing, so every puppy begins life on the strongest possible footing.

ii.

Beautiful Type

Gorgeous heavy coats and the shorter, cobby structure that defines a truly lovely Yorkshire and Biewer.

iii.

Exceptional Temperament

Even, confident, eager-to-please personalities, shaped through daily handling and genuine affection.

iv.

Family-Raised

Never a kennel. Every puppy is raised underfoot in our home with thoughtful, individual socialization.
