The opportunity

What you'd be doing

BreedWise helps serious dog breeders improve their breeding programs and businesses through veterinary-informed education, tools, coaching, and implementation support. We're already generating real interest on Facebook and Instagram — your job is to make sure none of it slips through the cracks.

You'll manage incoming conversations, engage interested breeders, understand what they need, and guide the right people to the right next step across our three programs:

  • The Profitable Breeder (TPB) — $97/month membership: assessments, education, tools, and weekly guidance.
  • BreedWise Academy — structured coaching, accountability, and guided implementation.
  • BOSS — premium, hands-on implementation support.

Your job is not to pressure anyone into buying. It's to respond quickly, understand their needs, create genuine interest, and move qualified people toward the right next step.

Your mission

Turn attention into opportunities

Turn BreedWise's existing social attention into qualified conversations, paid TPB memberships, and attended Academy & BOSS appointments. You'll be measured on response speed, conversation quality, qualified opportunities, consistent follow-up, and attributed results — not on how many messages you send.

What the day looks like

Your core responsibilities

  • Monitor assigned Facebook & Instagram messages and respond promptly to inbound questions.
  • Engage with comments that show interest and continue conversations our automations start.
  • Qualify prospects — their breeding stage, main challenge, the outcome they want, and their timeline.
  • Explain TPB and point suitable breeders to the membership; book qualified prospects onto approved sales calls for Academy & BOSS.
  • Confirm appointments, send reminders, and hand off cleanly to the closing team.
  • Keep the CRM accurate — ownership, status, notes, and attribution — and follow up on time.
  • Escalate customer-service, veterinary, or sensitive concerns to the right person.

How commissions work

  • TPB — $20 per member you bring in: $10 after their first monthly payment, and $10 more after their second — so you're rewarded for attracting members who stay.
  • Academy & BOSS — 3% of the cash actually collected on enrollments you're credited for. Because our high-ticket programs are paid in installments, your commission is paid as each payment comes in (e.g. Academy pays about $45 per installment, up to $135; BOSS about $60 per installment, up to $240).
  • Booking a call doesn't earn commission on its own — commission is earned when a credited sale closes and payment is collected. You'll always be credited fairly from the CRM record, with one primary setter per opportunity.
What a strong month can look like: on top of your base, bringing in 15 new TPB members, 10 members reaching their second payment, 2 Academy enrollments and 1 BOSS enrollment would add roughly $760 in commissions — and that grows as your pipeline and close rate grow.

What we'll expect

Response & performance standards

During your agreed coverage windows (these are targets we'll fine-tune together as volume settles):

  • New inbound sales DMs: aim to respond within 15 minutes.
  • Relevant public comments: reviewed and addressed within 60 minutes.
  • High-intent leads prioritized for immediate qualification or handoff; active conversations followed through on time.
  • As a planning reference, a full coverage day looks like roughly 30–40 meaningful conversations and 2–3 qualified bookings once systems and lead volume support it.
  • 100% CRM accuracy on qualified opportunities, booked appointments, and attributed sales — honest records, always.

You're responsible for your assigned coverage period, not 24/7 monitoring. Messages outside your window are handled in the next one. Targets are set after your first 30 days, based on real performance and demand.

How we work

Professional standards

  • Communicate professionally and respectfully; never misrepresent BreedWise or guarantee breeding, veterinary, or financial results.
  • No unauthorized discounts or payment promises; protect every prospect's information.
  • Follow approved scripts and adapt them naturally; avoid spam and excessive follow-up.
  • Respect a prospect's decision to decline, keep all records inside BreedWise systems, and raise problems early.
