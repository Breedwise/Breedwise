Our Story

Twelve years,
one purpose.

Blessed Beyond Dobermans was never meant to be a kennel. It grew out of a love for this breed and a conviction that a Doberman, raised right, is the most devoted companion a family can know.

Pueblo West, Colorado

It started at home, and it has stayed there.

For twelve years we have raised Doberman Pinschers the way we believe they are meant to be raised: inside our home, underfoot, in the middle of everything. Not in runs, not in a barn. Our puppies hear the dishwasher and the doorbell, feel different floors under their feet, and are handled by people of every age from their very first days.

That is not a marketing line. It is the single biggest reason our families tell us their Doberman settled in so quickly and so completely.

What We Breed For

Temperament is everything.

A Doberman will share your home, your routines, and your children's childhoods. That is why temperament comes first in every pairing we make, ahead of color, ahead of show points. We want confidence without nervousness, loyalty without anxiety, and the off-switch to simply be a gentle presence in the room.

Health follows close behind. Every parent is Embark DNA tested and screened, because longevity and soundness are not negotiable when a dog is going to be loved for a lifetime.
