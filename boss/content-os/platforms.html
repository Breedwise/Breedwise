The Platforms

Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok. Four platforms, four jobs. Know what each one is for, then master one and spread everywhere.
Set it up. There are four platforms that matter: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok. Each represents a different style of content and a different job in your business. We’ll break down what each is for, the post styles that belong on each, and then how to master one and repurpose that content across all of them.

Think of them as one journey, not four separate chores. TikTok gets strangers to discover you. Instagram is where they follow and build trust. Facebook is where serious, often older buyers gather and share. YouTube is where the most serious buyers go deep and convert. Reach at the top, trust at the bottom.
IG
Platform 01

Instagram — your storefront

Visual-first and relationship-driven. Your brand’s home base, where attention turns into DMs and inquiries.
Reels — reach & discovery, hook strangers
Carousels — teach and earn saves
Feed posts — your polished portfolio
Stories — daily behind-the-scenes & DMs
Highlights — evergreen: available pups, reviews, process
For: building the brand and converting attention into inquiries.
Instagram is your storefront — visual-first and relationship-driven. Reels are for reach and discovery. Carousels teach and get saved. Feed posts are your polished portfolio. Stories are your daily, human, behind-the-scenes layer where inquiries actually happen in the DMs. Highlights keep your best evergreen content — available puppies, reviews, your process — pinned for new visitors.
FB
Platform 02

Facebook — where buyers live

An older, higher-intent, local audience that actually shares. Where word-of-mouth spreads.
Groups — breed & local communities
Longer posts — stories told with photos
Shares — word-of-mouth reach
Events & availability — litters and openings
For: reaching real, ready buyers and local word-of-mouth.
Facebook is where buyers live — often a bit older, higher intent, and local. It rewards community: breed and local groups, longer story-style posts with photos, and shares that spread your name through word-of-mouth. Post your litters and availability as events and updates. This is where a lot of actual sales conversations start.
YT
Platform 03

YouTube — your authority library

Searchable and evergreen. It builds trust and ranks in search while you sleep.
Long-form — breed guides, facility tours, day-in-the-life
Shorts — discovery from repurposed clips
Playlists — an organized library buyers binge
Search — found by people researching the breed
For: deep trust and converting the most serious buyers.
YouTube is your authority library. Long-form videos — breed guides, facility tours, a day in the life — build deep trust. Shorts pull discovery from repurposed vertical clips. Playlists turn it into a library serious buyers binge. And because it’s searchable, people researching your breed find you for years. This converts your most serious buyers.
TT
Platform 04

TikTok — the discovery engine

The fastest organic reach there is. Raw, fast, and personality-first.
Short video — hooks, trends, fast cuts
Series — keep them coming back
Behind-the-scenes — authentic and unpolished
Personality — you are the differentiator
For: top-of-funnel awareness and getting discovered fast.
TikTok is the discovery engine — the fastest organic reach available. It’s raw, fast, and personality-first: strong hooks, trends, series that bring people back, and unpolished behind-the-scenes. You don’t need it perfect; you need it real. This is pure top-of-funnel awareness that feeds everything else.
Focus first

Master one platform first

Trying to win all four at once is how most breeders burn out and win none. Go deep on one.
IG FB YT TT
Pick the one that fits you, and become great at it.
The mistake is spreading thin across all four and being mediocre everywhere. Master one platform first — the one that fits your strengths and where your buyers are — and get genuinely good at it. Depth on one beats a weak presence on four.
Then expand

Create once, spread everywhere

1 content stylemastered on one platform
▸ Reels on Instagram
▸ Shorts on YouTube
▸ Posted to TikTok
▸ Shared into Facebook
The same vertical video works on all four. Master the style, then multiply it.
Once you’ve mastered one platform, take the style of content you’ve learned to make and spread it across all of them. A single vertical video becomes a Reel, a Short, a TikTok, and a Facebook share. You’re not creating four times the work — you’re multiplying one strong piece across every platform.
The big idea

Master one. Spread to all.

Know what each platform is for, go deep on one until you’re great, then repurpose that content style everywhere. One journey, four jobs, one content engine.
