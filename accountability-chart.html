What the chart reveals (structure, not blame)

Two people hold 12 of 19 seats. The structural risks, in priority order:

  1. Austin is Visionary + Integrator + Head of Growth. The person setting the 3-year vision is also running weekly ops and owning revenue — the classic bottleneck. First backfill: Integrator, then Head of Growth, so the Visionary seat is freed.
  2. Jennifer is Director of CS + Head of Ops & Finance at the same time — two division-head seats across different divisions. These should split as the next hire.
  3. Finance & Accounting and Admin/HR sit on Austin — lowest-leverage seats for a CEO. Cheapest, fastest things to delegate (the Admin/HR seat's own North Star is literally "fill open seats").

The mapping above is intentionally left as-is. The point of the chart is that each of these seats already has a clear North Star — so the moment you hire, the new person knows exactly what they own on day one.
