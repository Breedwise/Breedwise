Health & Breeding Standards

Bred to a standard,
not a shortcut

The Boerboel is a guardian built to work. We breed to protect what the breed is — sound in body, steady in mind — and we document it, so families can trust what they're bringing home.

Our Standard

Six commitments behind every litter

01

Temperament first

We breed for the confident, stable, family-safe guardian temperament the Boerboel is meant to have. A beautiful dog with the wrong mind is not a Stamyna dog.

02

Health, tested & shown

Our dogs are health-tested and their results disclosed to you — OFA (hips & heart), Embark, and Animal Genetics. We breed to reduce risk, not hide it.

03

Structure & soundness

Correct movement, bone, and proportion matter for a dog this size. We select for dogs that can move, age, and live well.

04

Breed type

We breed toward the true Boerboel standard — the head, substance, and presence that make the breed what it is — and appraise our dogs through SABBS.

05

Responsible pairings

Every pairing is planned for a reason: to strengthen temperament, health, and type — never to simply produce puppies.

Lifetime accountability

A word on responsible ownership

The dog we breed, and the owner we look for

We raise Boerboels as devoted family guardians — not as intimidation or liability. A well-bred Boerboel is confident because it is stable, protective because it is bonded, and safe because it is raised and led well. That's the dog we breed, and the owner we look for.
