FAQs

Got a Question?

What's the difference between The Profitable Breeder and BreedWise Academy?

TPB is built for clarity. Academy is built for personalized implementation. TPB helps you understand what's missing and what to prioritize; Academy goes deeper with assessment, a personalized roadmap, implementation support, and ongoing measurement.

Where should I start?

If you want education, tools, and clarity around what your program needs next, start with The Profitable Breeder. If you're ready for personalized implementation support, apply for Academy.

Does BreedWise replace my veterinarian?

No. BreedWise veterinary education helps breeders understand what to monitor, keep better records, prepare for complications, and communicate more effectively with their veterinary team. It does not replace veterinary diagnosis or treatment.

Will this work for my breed?

BreedWise teaches principles and systems that can apply across breeding programs, while individual breeding, health, genetic, and veterinary decisions still depend on breed, dogs, circumstances, and professional veterinary guidance.

What if I'm not sure which program is right for me?

Complete the BreedWise Program Scorecard and we'll help point you toward the appropriate starting point.
