The Effective Executive — opening script

"I want to introduce a principle we're going to start operating by across BreedWise. I've been studying The Effective Executive by Peter Drucker, and one of the biggest lessons is that being busy and being effective are not the same thing.

Every one of us has a lot we could work on. But what matters is identifying the contribution that will make the greatest difference to BreedWise this week.

For the next 90 days, our biggest priority is building a predictable sales engine that brings the right breeders into our programs and delivers on what we promise them.

Each Monday, every person identifies their single most important weekly contribution, the metric that measures it, and anything they need from another team member.

This isn't about doing only one thing — we still have our regular responsibilities. It's about knowing which result matters most when priorities compete. By Friday, we should be able to look at the numbers and know whether we made meaningful progress."
