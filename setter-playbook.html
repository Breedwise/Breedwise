Setter Playbook

The standard operating procedure for setting Academy applications. This is how Regina (and Yasmin) turn an application into a booked Fit Call on Austin's calendar. Follow it every time and the numbers take care of themselves.

You have one job: get qualified applicants on Austin's calendar, fast.

01

What a setter does (and doesn't)

✅ Your job

  • Speed: be first to reach every new application, ideally within minutes.
  • Confirm fit: make sure they're a real, operating breeder who's serious and can invest.
  • Book the Fit Call with Austin, and make sure they show.
  • Log everything cleanly in CC360 so Austin walks into the call prepared.

🚫 Not your job

  • Do NOT pitch or sell the Academy. You never quote the $6,000 or explain the whole program. That's Austin's call.
  • Don't answer deep "what's included / how much" questions in detail — tease and defer: "That's exactly what Austin walks you through on the call."
  • Don't chase unqualified people. If they're not a fit, disposition and move on.

Mental model: you're the concierge who reviewed their application and is personally getting them a seat with Austin. Warm, fast, in control. Not a salesperson.

03

Your day, structured

The workflow does the reminding, but you drive. Work in focused blocks.

First thing
Clear overnight applications — every fresh lead gets a call + text before you do anything else. Speed is the whole game.
Morning block
Work your call tasks in CC360 (the cadence assigns them to you). Dial in order of newest + hottest first.
Midday
Confirm every Fit Call booked for the next 24h (quick text/call) to protect show rate. Follow up on no-answers from the morning.
Afternoon block
Second round of dials on today's + yesterday's leads. Send the day's ride-along texts/emails to anyone you haven't reached.
End of day
Every lead has a next step or a disposition. Nothing sits untouched. Update notes so Austin's calls tomorrow are prepped.

Calling hours

Work leads 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM Eastern, Mon–Sat. The system already holds call tasks to this window, so don't force calls outside it.
04

How leads reach you

  1. A breeder submits the Academy application. It's scored instantly.
  2. They land in CC360 tagged academy-qualified and assigned to you (owner-regina).
  3. The call cadence workflow fires and starts dropping call tasks + texts/emails on your list.
  4. Their whole application is on the contact record — read it before you dial. Breed, biggest challenge, current pricing, and whether they said yes to investing are all there.
  5. You call, confirm fit, and book the Fit Call. The lead moves to Qualified – No Booking while you work it, then Enrollment Call Booked when you set it.

Read before you dial

Pull up the contact and glance at: their name, breed, biggest challenge, current puppy price, and tier. Opening a call already knowing their situation is the difference between a setter and a telemarketer.
05

The setter call — the frame

Five beats: open, confirm, dig, transition, book. Keep it 5–8 minutes. You lead, they answer.

1 · Open (pattern + permission)

You"Hey {name}? This is Regina with BreedWise — you just put in an application for the Academy, so I'm reaching out personally. Did I catch you at an okay time for a few minutes?"

2 · Confirm (why they applied)

You"I read through your application — I see you're breeding {breed}. In your own words, what made you raise your hand and apply right now?"
Let them talk. This is where they sell themselves.

3 · Dig (confirm the fit — do NOT sell)

Quietly confirm the three things that make them a fit for Austin's time:

  • Operating breeder: "How many litters are you doing a year right now?"
  • Real problem: "You mentioned {their biggest challenge} — how much is that costing you right now?"
  • Serious + able: "If Austin looks at your program and there's a clear path forward, are you in a position to invest in fixing it?" (They already answered yes to $1,500 on the app — you're confirming, not re-selling.)

4 · Transition (hand them to Austin)

You"Okay — based on what you're telling me, this is exactly the kind of program Austin can help. What he does on the call is look at where you are, find the biggest gaps, and lay out whether the Academy is the right fit to get you there. It's about 15 minutes, no pressure."

5 · Book (assume the close)

You"I've got some time on his calendar tomorrow — does morning or afternoon work better for you?"
Offer two options, not "when are you free." Get it on the calendar while you're on the phone. Confirm the email + number so reminders reach them.

Then lock it in

Send the confirmation immediately, tell them to expect a reminder, and set the expectation: "Come with your current pricing, your breed, and the one thing you most want to fix — Austin will take it from there."
06

Texts & voicemails

Use these when you don't reach them live. The workflow sends some automatically; send these personally when you're working the lead.

First text (with your first call)

Hey {name}, Regina with BreedWise here — got your Academy application and tried to reach you. When's a good 5 min to connect? I'll get you set up with Austin.

No answer, mid-cadence

{name}, still want to get you in front of Austin for your {breed} program. What's better today — morning or afternoon?

Voicemail (keep it short)

"Hey {name}, it's Regina with BreedWise about your Academy application. Give me a call back when you get a sec, or shoot me a text — I want to get you scheduled. Talk soon."
07

Objection handling

They sayYou say
"How much is it?""Great question — it depends on your program, which is exactly what Austin figures out on the call. That's why it's a fit call, not a pitch."
"Just send me info.""Totally fair — the honest answer is the 'info' is a look at YOUR program, and that only happens on the call. It's 15 minutes. Morning or afternoon?"
"I'm not sure I can afford it.""That's part of what the call is for — if it's not the right time, Austin will tell you straight. No pressure. Worst case you walk away with a clearer plan."
"I need to talk to my partner.""Smart — bring them on the call. When are you both free?"
"I'm too busy right now.""I hear you, breeding doesn't slow down. It's 15 minutes and it's about making the busy pay off. Even {day} evening?"

Golden rule: every objection ends by going back to booking the call. You're not debating — you're steering to Austin.

08

The handoff to Austin

A booked call is only half your job. Austin should open every Fit Call already knowing the person. Before the call, log a short note on the contact:

Handoff note template

Breed: ___ · Litters/yr: ___ · Biggest challenge: ___ · Current price: ___ · Why they applied: ___ · Can invest: yes/confirmed · Watch-outs: ___

  • Confirm the Fit Call is on Austin's calendar with the Zoom link.
  • Make sure the opportunity is on Enrollment Call Booked.
  • Text them the "come prepared" line so they show ready.
