Clear Before You Reserve

How reserving works

A deposit holds your chosen puppy. Each puppy's full price and deposit are shown above, before you commit to anything.

1

Meet & Match

Review photos and notes, then talk with us, by phone or live video, to make sure the fit is right.

2

Place a Deposit

Reserve your puppy with the deposit shown. We then send the health guarantee and current records.

3

Sign the Contract

Review and sign the written agreement, and we confirm the balance and homecoming plan.

4

Come Home

Pick up in person, or a pet nanny flies with your puppy and meets you at the airport.
