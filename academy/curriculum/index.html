The year, quarter by quarter

What you move through.

Each quarter stacks on the last, clinical foundation first, then the business that turns it into premium demand, then the systems that let it run without you.

Q1

Foundations of a clinical-grade program

Program design, health testing and genetics done to a clinical standard, the groundwork every later decision rests on.

You leave with: a defined breeding plan and health-testing standard for your program.
Q2

Reproduction, whelping & neonatal care

Breeding timing and fertility, safe whelping, neonatal care, and knowing exactly when to escalate to your vet, taught by the specialists.

You leave with: protocols for timing, whelping and the first critical weeks.
Q3

Premium positioning & the right buyers

Brand, buyer screening, waitlists and pricing, how to be chosen by the right families at the price your standard earns.

You leave with: a positioning, screening and pricing system for your program.
Q4

Systems, scale & certification

The Kennel OS, your vet referral network, and a capstone kennel audit that earns the certification. Built to run without you.

You leave with: your program on systems, and a certification you earned.
