Education

A living curriculum, one topic at a time.

Breeding a program the right way takes more than one skill. The Academy walks you through the whole path in order, from the standard you breed to, all the way to the systems that sell it. New modules are added every week, so the path keeps growing with the craft.

The learning path

Everything a serious program runs on.

  1. Purposeful Breeding

    Why you breed and to what standard, the vision and ethics that guide every pairing you make.

  2. Veterinary Relationships

    Build a true partnership with your vet, one that catches problems early and sends clients back to you.

  3. Genetics

    Testing, pedigrees and COI, how to pair for health and type instead of guessing.

  4. Health Testing

    OFA, genetic panels and the screening that proves your dogs are sound, and lets you charge like it.

  5. Reproduction

    Breeding timing, fertility, artificial insemination and breeding management done right.

  6. Neonatal Care & Whelping

    Safe whelping and the neonatal care that keeps more puppies alive and litters on schedule.

  7. Business Setup

    Structure your program like a real business: entity, accounts, pricing and the operations that make it run.

  8. Contracts & Agreements

    Puppy contracts, health guarantees, deposits and co-ownership, protecting your program and your buyers.

  9. Sales

    Positioning, buyer screening and premium pricing, getting paid what your program is worth.

  10. Branding

    A brand the right families trust and remember, so you're chosen before the conversation starts.

  11. Social Media Marketing

    The content and cadence that grow an audience of real buyers and build a waitlist.

  12. Advertising

    Paid strategies that put your program in front of the right buyers at the right time.

  13. AI Automations

    Systems that handle the admin, inquiries, follow-up and records, so you can focus on breeding.

…and more, added every week. The curriculum is a living thing, it grows as the craft does.
