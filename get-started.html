Questions
Before you apply

How does the guarantee actually work?

You do the work, we build the systems, and your waitlist collects deposits and sales. If that collected total doesn't reach what you paid for B.O.S.S. during your 120 days — while you've completed the program and shown up — you get your money back. You can watch your progress toward the goal on your dashboard the whole time.

What exactly gets built?

Your website, your CRM and lead system, and the waitlist, workflows, dashboards, templates and trackers your program needs — across both the breeding and the business side. Not access to software. Systems that are running.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. Our team configures the core systems with you and keeps the build moving. You bring your program and your effort; we bring the build and the structure.

I've tried ‘content’ and funnels before. Why is this different?

Because we build it with you instead of handing you a course and hoping. You leave with a website, a CRM and an operating system that fills litters — not a folder of videos and a to-do list.

How much time does it take?

It's a 120-day commitment with formal reviews at Day 30, 60, 90 and 120, strategy sessions, and a private channel so you can get unstuck fast. The more you show up, the more gets built.

Is this for me?

B.O.S.S. is for breeders who are done learning and ready to build — who'll show up, provide what we need, and do the work over 120 days. It's by application so every member gets built properly. The quickest way to find out is the form below.
