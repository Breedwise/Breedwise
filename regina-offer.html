Your role

What you own

You own BreedWise's outbound phone conversations — reaching breeders, qualifying them, and moving the right ones toward the right program. You've shown you can run the role reliably; now we build on it.

  • Work your assigned outbound lists and warm leads by phone during your coverage windows.
  • Qualify each breeder — their stage, their challenge, the outcome they want, and their timeline.
  • Book qualified prospects onto sales calls, confirm and remind them so they show.
  • Keep the CRM accurate — ownership, status, notes, and sales attribution.
  • Follow up consistently and surface high-intent opportunities quickly.

You're measured by qualified opportunities and collected revenue — not by hours or activity alone.
