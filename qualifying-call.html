BreedWise · Qualifying Call

NEPQ · 7-belief syntax

Qualify in 10 minutes. Book the fit.

Run top to bottom. Read the gold lines as written, capture the short answers, let the verdict at the bottom tell you whether to book. Talk to them breeder to breeder — you're not pitching, you're seeing if it's a fit. Build the dream before you ever mention money. Let them talk 80% of the time. The gold lines are the must-asks; the faint optional probes are only if you have room inside 10 minutes.

No pitch · build desire · then test the fit Permission → Context → Question Goal: financially qualified + urgent + decision-maker → book
0

Open & disarm

breeder to breeder
Take the pressure off and earn the right to ask. No pitch energy.
"Hey [name], really glad you made the time. Quick thing before we start — I'm not here to pitch you anything. We work with a lot of breeders, and honestly what each person needs is different, so I just want to understand your program and see if we're even the right people to help. Sound fair?"
"And what made you [reach out / take the scorecard / watch the case study]? What were you hoping to sort out?"
1

What they want to fix

core
Find the real gap, in their words. Don't move on until you've got it.
"So when you look at your program right now — where's it not quite where you want it yet? If you could fix one thing about the business side, what would move the needle most?"
"Anything else sitting right alongside that one?"
2

Their program

context · credibility
Enough to understand the program and show you speak breeder. Keep it quick.
"Tell me about your program — what are you raising, how many litters a year, and what are your pups going for right now?"
"And how are families finding you and getting on your list today — word of mouth, social, repeat buyers? Walk me through it."
Show you get it — reference health testing, placements or whelping naturally where it fits. Breeders trust people who understand the craft.
3

The real picture

dig · gently
Understand it, then put real numbers to it — warmly, not like an audit.
"Tell me more about [the problem] — what's that actually looked like for you lately?"
"Let's put some numbers to it so I get the full picture — over the last couple months, how many pups did you place, and around what price? … And is that where you want to be, or are you leaving money on the table?"
"How long's it been sitting around this level?"
"And when it stays like this — what's the real cost for you? Is it the income, the stress of scrambling every litter, not being able to charge what your dogs are truly worth? What's the weight of it?"
4

What's held it back

doubt
"Here's what I'm curious about — you clearly know the dog side cold. So what do you think's really been holding the business side back from where you want it?"
"Have you tried to crack it before — a mentor, a course, figuring it out solo? Anything going now?"
5

Why now

urgency
Find genuine urgency — no pressure tactics. Breeders feel manipulation instantly.
"You've probably known about this a while — so why is now the moment you decided to actually do something about it? What shifted?"
"Anything else making now the time?"
"And if it just stayed exactly like this for the next year or two — how would you feel about that?"
5
Low urgency. Keep nurturing the vision, or follow up later — don't book a soft lead onto the closer's calendar.
6

The vision

desire · lead here
Build the dream BEFORE money. Reputation, waitlist and pride are a breeder's real fuel.
"Let's get to the fun part. If we fixed [the problem] and your program ran the way you know it could — what does that look like? What kind of breeder do you want to be known as?"
"Paint it for me — a full waitlist? Pricing your pups at what they're truly worth? Choosing the homes instead of chasing buyers?"
"And what would that free up for you — more time with the dogs and your family, less stress, a program you're genuinely proud of?"
7

The number & the fit

must-get
Money AFTER the dream, tied to their goal. Test capacity — never quote a price.
"When you picture that version of your program — what would it need to be bringing in for it to feel like you've truly made it? What's the number?"
"Why that number — what's it really about for you?"
"Love it. And look — I'm not going to throw a price at you today, that's for the next conversation. But so I don't waste your time: if we showed you a clear path to [their goal] and it was obviously worth it, are you in a spot where you could invest in making it happen this year?"
Not in a position to invest. Be honest and kind — keep nurturing, don't book a call they can't act on.
8

Who's in on it

decision
One respectful question. Frame the partner as a teammate, never an obstacle.
"If you decided to go for it, is that a call you'd make on your own — or is there a partner you'd want in on it with you?"
If partner: "Totally get it — would it make sense to have them on the next call too, so you're both looking at the plan together?"
Bring the partner onto the next call — frame it as "so you're both in the loop," and book both.
9

Tie-down & book

book the fit
"Honestly, this has been a great conversation — I feel like I really get where you are and where you want to go. Anything about your program or your goals I didn't ask that you think I should know?"
"And realistically — the breeders who get results fastest put in just a few focused hours a week. That feel doable for you?"
Book it: "Here's my honest take: based on everything, I think we can genuinely help you get to [their goal]. The next step is a deeper call with [Austin] who'll map out exactly how — no obligation, just the plan. Let's lock it in right now while we're together. What's your week look like?"
!

When to switch to Goals-First

read me
Default to Problems-First. Switch here only when:
· It's a new direction for them (just getting into breeding, or a big pivot).
· They can't diagnose the problem, give the wrong one ("my website"), or say "I don't know where to start."
· They're confused — defaulting to "so what do you actually want?" cuts through it.
1

Pivot to the goal

(They're unclear or new) → "You know what, let's start with the end in mind instead — it'll be easier. If everything went right, what are you actually hoping to build here?"
2

The vision & the fit

desire first
"What kind of breeder do you want to be known as?" · "What would the program need to bring in for it to feel like you've made it — and why that number?" · "Beyond the money — what do you want it to give you?"
Then the fit (no price): "If we showed you a clear path to that and it was obviously worth it, could you invest in making it happen this year?"
Capture into the same fields (the dream, monetary goal, non-monetary, financial) — scroll up or fill after the call.
3

Where they are now

"And where are you today with it — what are you working with right now?" → gently dig the gap between here and the dream.
"What do you think's really been the thing stopping you from getting there on your own?"
"Tried anything before? Working with anyone now? … And who'd be part of a decision like this with you?"
4

The two keys to success

authority + insight
Build authority, give real insight, and surface healthy doubt (they won't have both dialed in).
"Can I share something that might help? To build a breeding program that actually pays you what your dogs are worth, it really comes down to two things:"
1 — A program and brand people happily pay a premium for (your quality, your reputation). 2 — A reliable way to fill your waitlist (demand you can turn on, not luck each litter).
"On the brand and premium side — where do you feel you're at? … And on consistently filling your list — do you have a system, or is that the piece you'd want help with?"
They'll almost always say demand is the gap. That's your bridge — then book the fit call.
5

Tie-down & book

"This has been great — anything about your program or your goals I didn't ask that I should know?" → then book the deeper call with [Austin] to map out the plan.
+

Setter notes

Super-basic transition if you ever freeze: "I don't feel like I have any more questions. Based on what you told me, we can definitely help. Where do you want to go from here — I can walk you through exactly how we'd help you get to [goal] if you'd like?"
