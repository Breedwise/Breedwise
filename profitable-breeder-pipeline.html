The Lead Pipeline

Every conversation, from first contact through the Program Assessment to joining The Profitable Breeder — in one pipeline, so we can see who is actually moving and where it stalls.

The operating rule

The scorecard is the line. A conversation is not a lead until the Program Assessment is complete. We measure lead volume by assessments completed, not by how many chats we have open.

Conversation
Anyone we're talking to

Inbound, DM, comment, referral. Interest, but unqualified.

Qualified lead
A completed assessment

They finished the Program Assessment. We have scored results and a readiness verdict.

Member
Enrolled in TPB

They joined The Profitable Breeder.

Where the pipeline leaks:
Conversation → assessment weak — we're not getting people to complete the scorecard. Fix the invite framing and the follow-up on started-but-not-finished.
Assessment → debrief weak — completions aren't being turned into booked debriefs fast enough.
Debrief → offer weak — we're not connecting their assessment gaps to what TPB solves.
Offer → enrolled weak — a value, trust or certainty problem at the offer.

Make it real in CC360

  • Build a CC360 pipeline named The Profitable Breeder with these eight stages plus Nurture and Not a Fit.
  • Send the assessment link with the lead's email pre-filled — scorecard.html?email=&name= — so a completion maps back to their contact and can auto-advance the stage.
  • The scorecard already tags tpb-scorecard-complete plus a verdict (academy-ready / watch / nurture) on completion. Use that tag to move a contact across the line, and the verdict to order the debrief queue.
  • In reporting, define lead = a contact with the tpb-scorecard-complete tag. Everything before it is a conversation, not a lead.
  • The verdict decides urgency: academy-ready debriefs first, watch next, nurture stays in follow-up until something changes.
