FAQ

What actually happens during the 2 days?

It’s a working intensive, not a talk. Day 1 you diagnose your real numbers and design your pricing, value and demand. Day 2 you build your 12-month roadmap, 90-day plan and a 30-day next step — with Austin and Dr. Fontaine.

Who is this for?

Established breeders producing roughly 2–3+ litters per year who want stronger margins, qualified buyers and better systems. It’s not for first-litter breeders.

Is it live? Are recordings included?

Yes — live online, 11:00 AM–2:00 PM ET on Sept 26 & 27. Recordings are included with VIP only (lifetime access).

What’s the difference between GA and VIP?

Both get the full 2 days, templates and live Q&A. VIP adds two implementation labs, lifetime recordings, priority questions and a private 30-minute plan review with Austin — only 10 seats.

Is this veterinary training?

Dr. Fontaine teaches veterinary-backed educational material. It does not replace advice from your own veterinarian.

Refunds & transfers?
