How Academy works

A clear path, not a pile of courses.

Three phases, one standard: an intensive to get your program in shape, an ongoing membership that keeps it there, and a certification you actually earn.

  1. Phase 01
    The 120-Day Core

    Your first four months. We assess your program, set the priorities that matter, and install the clinical and business foundation with you — one step at a time.

    • Days 1–30 — Baseline assessment, your real litter economics, and an individual action plan
    • Days 31–60 — Install the essentials: health protocols, buyer qualification, clean records
    • Days 61–120 — Build consistency: a marketing cadence, follow-up, and financial tracking you'll keep
    You finish with a program that's measurably better run — not just a breeder who read more.
  2. Phase 02
    Ongoing Membership

    After the core, you roll into the Breedwise membership and keep getting better — for as long as it serves your program.

    • Live group coaching every week, with the team and faculty
    • Clinical standards kept current as the science moves
    • A private room of serious, established breeders
    • The certification track, worked at your pace
    The standard doesn't expire when a course ends — and neither does your support.
  3. Phase 03
    Earned Certification

    Certification you earn by demonstrating the standard through a real breeding cycle — which is why it takes most of a year. A credential you earn, never one that arrives with a receipt.

    • 80% across the module knowledge checks
    • A faculty-graded practical portfolio
    • A capstone kennel audit
    A Breedwise-issued certification, with published criteria and independent veterinary oversight.
The certification

Earned, overseen, and honest about what it is.

A Breedwise certification is a standard you meet, verified by people qualified to judge it — not a certificate that prints the day you pay. We publish exactly what it takes.

See who verifies it →

Published criteria

80% across the knowledge checks, a faculty-graded practical portfolio, and a capstone kennel audit — the same bar for everyone.

Independent oversight

The clinical standard is reviewed independently by board-certified veterinary specialists; the genetics by our veterinary geneticist.

What it is not

It's a Breedwise-issued credential — not a veterinary license, and not a guarantee of any outcome. You earn it; you don't buy it.

Why it holds up

When it goes sideways at 2am, you'll know what to do.

Timing, whelping, a neonate in trouble, a pairing you're unsure of — the moments that cost litters are the ones where guessing is most expensive. The clinical modules are built and taught by board-certified veterinary specialists, so when it matters you're working from the standard, not a hunch.
