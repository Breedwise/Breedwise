Revenue Is Not Profit

Selling the Litter Doesn’t Automatically Mean the Litter Was Profitable.

Money coming in feels like a win. But money in and money kept are not the same thing. Every litter has real costs:

Stud & service feesProgesterone & reproductive careTravel & shippingHealth testingVeterinary careC-section & emergency careWhelping suppliesFoodRegistrationMarketingYour time & laborPuppy careUnexpected complications

If you set your price by copying other breeders, you may not know if it even covers the care you want to give.

And it’s not only about price. If puppies show up before families are lined up, everything gets harder. You post more, answer more messages, deal with people who aren’t a good fit, hear “what’s your lowest?”, watch the puppies get older, and start to wonder if you should drop the price.

A price mistake can hit every puppy in the litter. A shortage of families shows up once the puppies are already here. A gap in your prep tends to show up right when the pressure is highest.

Another Litter Is an Expensive Place to Keep Guessing.

The Profitable Breeder Intensive helps you find those gaps before your next litter finds them for you.

Questions, Answered

Who is this for?

Breeders who already have litters, usually about 2 to 3 or more a year, and want better numbers, better prices, more good families, and better prep with vet-backed help. It is not a first-litter course.

I’ve been breeding for years. Will this be too basic?

No. Breeders with 7 to 13 years of experience have seen real changes after working with BreedWise. This assumes you already know how to breed, and works on the money and prep side many breeders were never taught.

Can this actually help me make more money?

We can’t promise money results. It helps you understand and improve the things that drive your business: your costs, your price, how you stand out, how you find families, and how you close. Your results depend on your program, breed, market, price, and follow-through.

Why does veterinary education belong in a profitability event?

Because at BreedWise, making money never comes before good care. A strong program needs both numbers that work and care done right. That is why Austin and Dr. Fontaine teach it together.

Is Dr. Fontaine replacing my veterinarian?

No. His part is teaching only. It helps you prepare better and work better with your own vet.

What happens if I miss part of the live event?

General Admission includes 14-day replay access. VIP includes lifetime access to the recordings. Dogs do not care about your calendar, so we built in a cushion.

What’s the difference between General Admission and VIP?

General Admission gives you the room, the tools, the working sessions, and your finished plan. VIP adds more one-on-one help and an expert review of your own program.

What do I actually leave with?

You leave with your finished Profitable Breeder Plan™, built around your own Working Litter. It pulls together what your litter costs and keeps, what to expect from the breeding, your price and its worth, your whelping and newborn prep, your 90-day plan to find families, how you handle families, your 12-month to-do list, and your first 30-day steps.

It is not a stack of worksheets you’ll forget. It is one plan for the program you’re actually running.
