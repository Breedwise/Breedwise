Our Story

Raised with love, purpose, and intention

My husband and I have been devoted to raising and breeding since 2005. What began as a love for this beautiful breed has grown into a lifelong passion and a commitment to doing our very best to preserve and improve the qualities we treasure most.

Over the years, we have worked diligently to produce puppies who are not only beautiful, with gorgeous heavy coats and the shorter, cobby structure we love, but most importantly, who are healthy, well-balanced, confident, and wonderfully socialized. For us, breeding has never been simply about producing puppies. It is about raising them with love, purpose, and intention.

Eight years ago, another family joined us in this journey, and our shared love for the breed has made this endeavor even more special. Together, we raise our puppies in a warm, loving, family-centered home where they are surrounded by people, handled with care, and given the individual attention they need to thrive.

Every puppy we raise carries with them a little piece of our hearts, and it is our hope that they bring your family the same love, joy, and companionship this incredible breed has brought into ours.

— Judith & Family
