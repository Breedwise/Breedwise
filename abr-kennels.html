Our Story

Faith, Family & Great Danes

ABR Kennels Boarding & Training Center is a family program in Glasgow, Kentucky — Home of the Rain Danes. We raise AKC Great Danes and English Mastiffs the way we'd want our own family's dog raised: underfoot, well-socialized, and loved.

Every puppy leaves with a strong foundation and lifetime support — because our families are part of ours.
