What you need to know

BreedWise was built to give serious breeders the business and veterinary systems most programs were never built with — pricing, demand, buyer process, and the health-first standards behind them. It's led by founder Austin Shanks and reproduction specialist Dr. Emmanuel Fontaine.

Our members have raised their pricing, built qualified waitlists, and started running their programs like real businesses — several moving their minimum puppy price from roughly $2,500 toward $5,000+, and others selling at their program's highest price ever within weeks of joining.

Now we're looking for a few more established breeders — already producing litters — who are ready to do the same.
