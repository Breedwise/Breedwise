The 4 daily responsibilities

1 · Ideation

Study what already works and turn signal into more signal.

  • Replicate — it works, make another version
  • Iterate — keep the concept, improve hook/angle/format
  • Innovate — test something genuinely new

Sources: winning BreedWise + breeder content, adjacent industries, hooks, formats, breeder questions, objections, comments worth filming, competitor gaps, trends.

2 · Creation

Idea → Hook → Script/framework → Creator assigned → Filmed → Edited → Approved → Scheduled
  • Austin breeder / business / founder
  • Emmanuel veterinary education
  • Yasmin brand / social / community

Every batch day already has topics, hooks, scripts, CTAs ready. They show up to film, not to figure out what to film.

3 · Distribution

Daily publishing with purpose: Attention → Trust → Education → Conversion.

Content → Engagement → DM/Lead → TPB → Academy

Not every post needs a CTA, but the system must create a path. Build ManyChat into the journey where appropriate.

4 · Delegation & accountability

You own the pipeline even when someone else owns the task.

Identify → Follow up → Escalate → Solve

Don't silently absorb missed work. If a dependency threatens the pipeline, surface it.

  • What lost?

  • What's blocking production?
