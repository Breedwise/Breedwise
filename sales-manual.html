Start here — everyone

The Sales OS is the team scoreboard: leads, bookings, calls, and sales measured against your Q3 targets — every number with a name on it. It's fed live from CC360 (scheduling) and Close (calls & notes).

📋 New here? Read How Sales Works first. It's the one-page standard — what a lead is, the 9 CC360 stages, source tags, and who owns what. This manual is how to use the OS; that page is how sales works.

Logging in

  1. Go to review.breedwise.io/sales
  2. Sign in with your Breedwise email + password (ask Austin if you need one).
  3. You'll see the Team Scoreboard; My Dashboard is your personal view.
🔁 The CRM is the source of truth. Leads sync in from CC360 + Close — the Leads tab is a live mirror. You work leads in the CRM (CC360 for scheduling/stage, Close for calls/notes), and the OS reflects it.

The tabs

Team Scoreboard
Volume vs target (leads, bookings, calls, sales) and the four conversion rates that matter, with the funnel + revenue.
My Dashboard
Your personal numbers, shaped to your role. (Founder sees full-team oversight here.)
Leaderboard
Every rep ranked — outbound, booked, shows, closed, close rate, cash.
Leads
The pipeline. View any lead to see its full history; Edit to update OS fields.
Q3 Targets
The hard-coded quarterly targets + each rep's daily outbound target.

Reading a lead

On the Leads tab, click View on any lead to open its history — notes from CC360 & Close, calls, appointments, and (for applicants) their full application. A tier badge shows application quality:

  • Hot ready to invest, urgent, serious — call these first.
  • Warm good fit, needs nurture / a payment plan.
  • Disqualified — not a fit (can't invest, just wants tips). Don't burn call time here.
The 3 moments of truth

The scoreboard is only as real as these. Mark them in the CRM every time:

  1. Booked — the call is set (CC360 handles this).
  2. Showed / No-show — mark it after every call.
  3. Won / Lost — disposition every opportunity.

Skip these and your close rate, show rate, and revenue all read blank — no matter how hard you worked.

Quick answers

  • A lead isn't showing? Leads sync from the CRM — new ones land within a few minutes.
  • Edited a lead but the CRM didn't change? The Leads tab is a one-way mirror for synced fields — make CRM changes in CC360/Close.
  • My numbers look low? Almost always means outcomes weren't logged. Mark showed/no-show and won/lost in Close.
  • Forgot your password? Ask Austin to reset it.
