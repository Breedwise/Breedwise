This Privacy Policy explains how Breedwise LLC ("BreedWise," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or apply to work with us.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us, such as your name, email, phone number, breed, and details about your program when you submit a form or application.
  • Usage information collected automatically, such as pages visited and general device information, through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry, review your application, and schedule calls.
  • To provide, improve, and personalize our services and communications.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example, scheduling, email, and CRM tools) and when required by law.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may unsubscribe from marketing at any time, by emailing support@breedwise.io.

Data retention & security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes described here and take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
