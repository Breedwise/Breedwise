Why We're Changing How I Work

My time was the bottleneck. Not anymore.

Three problems were capping our growth. This calendar is built to remove all three.

01

Delivery ate my calendar

Building tools, running low-ticket 1:1s, Academy group calls, masterclasses — all on me. No time left to sell or lead.

02

Delegation kept breaking

Not because the team can't — because there was no written standard and expectations were informal. If it isn't documented and measured, it isn't delegated.

03

Wrong program getting my energy

We over-deliver for the Academy (low ticket) and under-deliver for the Accelerator (high ticket). That's backwards — and it's where the money is.

The Shift

It starts with the offer: Academy ($5K/yr, DIY + light support) and Accelerator ($15K/yr, done-for-you) get distinct, non-overlapping deliverables — no more over-delivering the Academy and blurring the $10K gap. Academy becomes group-only, team-led, and recorded; the Accelerator earns my premium attention. My hours then sit on the three highest-leverage points — selling, Accelerator quality, and building the team that runs everything else.

The Operating Principles

Six rules this calendar runs on.

1
I'm out of Academy delivery.

Academy questions and sessions route to the team lead — not to me. I spot-check quality, I don't run it.

2
Delivery is built to a standard, not to me.

Every asset and session has a written SOP so anyone trained can run it consistently. I review and approve — I don't build.

3
Every delegated area has four things.

An owner, a written standard, a number it's measured by, and a weekly check-in. Missing one? It's not handed off yet.

4
Sales windows are sacred.

Mon–Thu, 1:00–4:30 is selling. No internal meetings, no interruptions. Max 5 calls a day — protected on both ends.

5
Async first.

Default to Loom or written. Live time with me is for decisions, coaching, and high-leverage delivery — not status updates.

6
Come with a recommendation.

Every escalation includes context, what you already tried, and what you think we should do. Bring me decisions, not open questions.

Rules of Engagement

How to work with my calendar.

✕
Don't book during sales windows or deep work.

Mon–Thu 1:00–6:00 & Sat 1:00–4:30 (sales) and 8:00–10:00 daily (deep work) are closed. Nothing internal goes here.

◐
Strategy sessions are earned, not open.

The 1.5-hr slot (Tue–Fri) only books for a brand-new Academy member or an Accelerator member. No other 1:1s go on my calendar — route everyone else to group support.

↳
Follow the escalation path.

Check the SOP → ask your lead → then bring it to me. Most things should never reach me.

⏱
Know my response windows.

I answer async at day's end and on Fridays — not in real time. Urgent + revenue-blocking is the only exception.

◎
Route Academy to the team.

Academy members and questions go to Client Success. Sending them to me slows everyone down.

☾
Sundays are off — completely.

Sunday is my life review with my S/O and recovery. No work, no calls, no exceptions. It's what makes the other six days sustainable.

▤
Book me in the right block.

Decisions → my Friday 1:1 slot. Delivery input → the review blocks. Sales help → the debrief window.

✎
If it's not written down, it's not done.

Every recurring task becomes an SOP. That's how it stays off my plate for good.

The 90-Day Handoff

How we get there — in three phases.

I don't drop everything at once. Over the quarter, delivery moves fully to the team and my build blocks convert into scale.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4

Document & Train

  • Every delivery task gets a written SOP
  • Heavy Thu/Fri investment in training the team
  • I still cover some delivery while standards are built
  • Scorecards go live for every owner
Phase 2 · Weeks 5–8

Shadow & Handoff

  • Team leads run delivery; I observe & coach
  • I exit 1:1s and Academy entirely
  • Closer takes a growing share of calls
  • Weekly check-ins replace me doing the work
Phase 3 · Weeks 9–12

The Team Owns It

  • I'm on masterclass + weekly Accelerator + sales + leadership only
  • Build blocks convert to scale — offers, partnerships, ad scaling
  • Accelerator delivered to a standard that sells itself
  • Saturday becomes optional
The whole point: $100K/month = 5 paid-in-full deals a week. Our close rate is already elite (~72%) — the leak isn't selling, it's follow-up and show rate. So the plan is: fix the offer so the Accelerator sells itself, enforce the setter and triage standards to plug the funnel, and free my time to compound on selling and Accelerator quality. Great delivery creates the testimonials and referrals that make sales easier and let us scale ads. Academy feeds the top. My job is to protect the three levers and hold the standard; your job is to own it.
