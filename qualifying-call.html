NEPQ · 7-belief syntax

Qualify in 10 minutes. Book the fit.

Run top to bottom. Read the gold lines as written, capture the short answers, and let the verdict at the bottom tell you whether to book. Two goals on every call: find out if/how we can help, and eliminate objections before the close. Stay brief — the prospect should talk 80% of the time.

Pain · Doubt · Cost · Desire · Money · Support · Trust Permission → Context → Question Goal: financially qualified + urgent + decision-maker
0

Open & frame

30 sec
Lower resistance and earn permission to ask questions.
"Hey [name], appreciate you jumping on. Before I tell you anything about us, I want to make sure this is even a fit — so do you mind if I ask you a few quick questions about your program?"
"And just so I know — what made you [fill out the scorecard / watch the case study / register]?"
1

Isolate the problem

core
Stay here until you know the real problem. Then put your shovel in the ground.
"When it comes to your breeding program right now — what's the biggest challenge? What's not working at the level it really should be?"
"Besides that — is there any other roadblock that's in the way right now?"
2

Background

context · brief
Just enough context to understand the program and whether we can help. Move fast.
"Got it. Real quick so I have context — what breed(s) do you raise, roughly how many litters a year, and what do your puppies usually go for?"
"And right now, how do people find you and buy — walk me through how a stranger becomes a buyer."
3

Current situation

dig here
Probe the problem, assess efforts, chunk down the money, widen the gap.
"Tell me more — what do you mean by that? … Why's that the biggest one? … How's that actually impacting your income / the program?"
"So what are you doing right now to [get buyers / fill the waitlist / raise prices]?"
Chunk down: "Let's look at the numbers — last couple months, how many puppies did you actually place? … At what price? … So revenue's around what? … Is that what you want to be charging, or what do you really want to be at?"
"How long's it been like this?"
Widen the gap: "That long — has that had an impact on you? In what way? … Does that put you in a tough spot with [income / your family / your time]?"
4

Doubt & what they've tried

belief
"Can I ask you honestly — what do you think's been keeping you from figuring this out on your own? What's in the way?"
"Have you tried anything to fix it before — courses, mentors, programs? Working with anyone now?"
5

Why now / Cost

urgency
This is where it gets real. Find out why NOW — and what happens if nothing changes.
"You've dealt with this a while — why is now the time you're finally doing something about it? What changed?"
"Any other reason this is important now?"
"Real talk — what's the plan if nothing changes? If you're in the same spot a year from now… would that be okay with you?"
If apathetic: "Whose decision is that? … Are you willing to settle for that? … If you're willing to settle, how do you expect me to help you as a client?"
5
Low urgency. Test it harder or move to nurture — don't book a soft lead onto the closer's calendar.
6

Support & decision-maker

belief
"Is there anyone else involved in a decision like this — a spouse or partner?"
"Are they supportive of you investing in the program? … Do they know you're on this call? … What would they think if they did?"
Partner needs to be on the next call. Set that expectation now and book both.
7

Desired situation

desire + money
"So if we fixed [problem] — what's the goal? What do you want this program to become?"
"What's the monetary goal — what do you want to be making from your breeding?" … "You've clearly thought about that number — why that number?"
"Long-term, what's the vision?"
Non-monetary: "Beyond the money — what do you want this to give you? The freedom, the lifestyle, the legacy?"
8

Financial qualifier

must-get
Don't quote the price. Test their capacity and willingness to invest in a real solution.
"If this were the right fit and we could actually get you to [goal] — are you in a position to invest in yourself and your program right now?"
Not financially qualified. Be honest — don't book a call they can't act on. Move to nurture.
9

Tie-down & transition

commit temp
"Feels like we covered a lot — is there anything I didn't ask that you think I should know?"
Time: "Let's say you went ahead with something that fixed [problem] — most of our breeders put in about a few focused hours a week to get there. Is that something you'd be willing to commit to?"
Partner: "And if we worked together, would [spouse/partner] be part of that decision?"
Book it: "Based on everything you told me, I'm confident we can help. The next step is a call with [closer] who'll walk you through exactly how — let's get you booked while we're on the phone."
!

When to switch to Goals-First

read me
Default to Problems-First. Switch here only when:
· It's a new direction for them (just getting into breeding, or a big pivot).
· They can't diagnose the problem, give the wrong one ("my website"), or say "I don't know where to start."
· They're confused — defaulting to "so what do you actually want?" cuts through it.
1

Isolate, then pivot to the goal

"What's the biggest challenge right now?" → (they're unclear) → "What might make more sense is to start with the end in mind. So ultimately — what's the goal?"
2

Desired situation

desire + money
"What's the goal?" · "What's the monetary goal — why that number?" · "Long-term vision?" · "Beyond money — the personal goal?"
Financial qualifier: "If it were the right fit, are you in a position to invest in getting there?"
Capture into the same fields above (monetary goal, vision, non-monetary, financial qualifier) — scroll up or fill after the call.
3

Current situation

"What do you do now — and do you like it?" → dig the pain or the "why change now."
Doubt: "What's been the biggest thing stopping you from getting this started yourself?"
Solution + support: "Tried anyone before? Working with anyone now?" · "Anyone else part of the decision — partner?"
4

Outline 1-2 keys to success

authority + doubt
Build authority, give insight, and create healthy doubt (they won't have both dialed in).
"Can I give you a little coaching for a second? To build a breeding program that actually pays you what it should, you really only need two things:"
1 — A program people want at a premium (your quality + a brand that commands the price). 2 — A reliable way to get buyers (demand you can turn on, not luck each litter).
"On your offer and brand — where do you feel you're at? … And on getting buyers on demand — do you have a system, or is that something you'd need help building?"
They'll almost always say they need help with demand. That's the gap — then transition.
5

Tie-down & transition

"Feels like we covered a lot — anything about your program or goals we didn't cover that I should know?" → then book the fit call.
+

Setter notes
