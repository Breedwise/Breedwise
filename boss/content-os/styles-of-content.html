Styles of Content

Your content toolbox. Twenty-four styles to pull from — so you never run out of ideas and every post has a job.
Use this as a reference. This module is your menu of content styles. Each has a name and a quick description you can expand on. The goal: whenever you sit down to create, pull from this list, mix styles, and never stare at a blank screen again. We’ll walk the four families — the dogs, your story, teaching & trust, and formats & reach.

Group the styles into four families: the dogs, you and your story, teaching and trust, and formats and reach. A strong feed rotates across all four — not just puppy photos. Pull one from each family across a week and your content stays varied, human, and valuable.
Family 01

The dogs

Puppy content — the scroll-stopper: newborns, playtime, go-home day
Adult dog content — studs, dams, retired dogs; structure & temperament
Litter updates & availability — announcements, “available now,” reservations
Transformations — week-by-week growth and then-and-now
Picking & go-home day — the emotional buyer moments
Dog personalities — each dog’s quirks and character
The dogs are your stars. Puppy content stops the scroll. Adult dog content shows the quality behind the program. Litter updates and availability drive inquiries. Transformations show growth over time. Picking and go-home day capture the emotion buyers want. And leaning into each dog’s personality makes your animals feel like characters people follow.
Family 02

You & your story

Filming yourself — talking to camera; your voice and authority
Real stories — the honest journey, wins and losses, as a narrative
Behind-the-scenes — the daily, unpolished reality of your program
Day-in-the-life — a full day running your kennel
Family & lifestyle — your family dynamic and culture
Your “why” — the purpose behind your program
People buy from people. Filming yourself builds authority and connection — it’s so powerful it has its own module. Real stories, told as a narrative, make you relatable. Behind-the-scenes and day-in-the-life show the real work. Family and lifestyle and your “why” give people a reason to root for you, not just your puppies.
Family 03

Teaching & trust

Educational — breed education, buyer tips, myth-busting
Health & care — testing, whelping, nutrition, vet care
Process content — how you raise, how to buy, what to expect
Facility & kennel tours — your setup and your standards
Testimonials — happy families, reviews, updates from their homes
Q&A / FAQ — the questions every buyer asks, answered
Teaching builds trust and authority. Educational content positions you as the expert. Health and care content proves your standards. Process content removes buyer fear by showing exactly how it works. Facility tours showcase your setup. Testimonials are social proof that sells for you. And Q&A content answers objections before they’re even asked.
Family 04

Formats & reach

Carousels — multi-image teaching that gets saved
Ads — paid reach straight to your ideal buyer
Trends & trending audio — ride momentum for discovery
Memes & relatable — humor that spreads
Lives — real-time Q&As and puppy reveals
Collaborations & UGC — features, partners, client-made content
These are distribution plays. Carousels get saved and teach. Ads put you in front of ideal buyers on demand. Trends and trending audio ride the algorithm for reach. Memes and relatable humor spread fast. Lives build real-time connection and reveal puppies. Collaborations and user-generated content borrow other audiences and give you free, trusted proof.
The big idea

You’ll never run out

Twenty-four styles, four families. Pull a few each week, rotate across all four, and repurpose your best ones. A full toolbox means a blank screen is never an excuse.
