Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026 · Last updated: July 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Breedwise Academy, LLC ("Breedwise," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit our websites and funnels (including pages under go.breedwise.io), submit a form, book or attend a call, or otherwise interact with our content, ads, emails, or text messages (collectively, the "Services").

The short version: We collect the details you give us (like your name, email, phone, and where you are in breeding) plus standard analytics, and we use them to deliver the case study, contact you about a call, run and measure our advertising, and improve our program. We don't sell your information for money. You can opt out of emails and texts at any time, and you can ask us to access or delete your data by emailing support@breedwise.io.

1.Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact & profile details: your name, email address, phone/mobile number, and information about your breeding program (for example, where you are in breeding, breeds, litter counts, pricing, and goals) that you enter into our forms, applications, or the booking scheduler.
  • Communications: messages, replies, and information you share with us by email, text, on a call, or through our scheduler.

Information collected automatically

  • Device & usage data: IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages viewed, links clicked, referring/exit pages, and dates/times of access.
  • Marketing attribution: UTM parameters, click identifiers (such as a Meta fbclid), and any affiliate/referral code (ref) present in the link you arrived through.
  • Cookies & similar technologies: as described in the "Cookies & tracking" section below.

Information from third parties

  • Advertising & referral partners: ad platforms (such as Meta) and affiliates/partners may share limited information about how you reached us.
  • Service providers: our scheduling, CRM, and communications tools may enrich your record with activity you take within those tools (e.g., booking, attending, or rescheduling a call).

2.How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Deliver the free case study and related content you requested;
  • Contact you about, schedule, confirm, remind you of, and follow up on your call (by email, phone, and text);
  • Provide, operate, personalize, and improve the Services;
  • Send marketing and educational communications (which you can opt out of at any time);
  • Deliver, target, measure, and improve our advertising, and build audiences (see "Advertising & the Meta Pixel");
  • Attribute leads and bookings to the correct campaign or affiliate;
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, and debug our systems; and
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms.

Where required by law, we rely on your consent (e.g., for marketing texts), the need to perform a contract or take steps at your request, and our legitimate interests in operating and growing our business.

3.Cookies & tracking technologies

We and our partners use cookies, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies to run the site, remember your first-touch referral source, understand traffic, and measure and improve advertising. Some are necessary for the site to function; others are analytics or advertising technologies.

You can control cookies through your browser settings and limit interest-based ads through the tools listed in the next section. Blocking some technologies may affect how the site works.

4.Advertising & the Meta Pixel

Our pages include the Meta (Facebook) Pixel. It helps us understand actions you take (such as viewing a page, opting in, or booking a call), measure ad performance, and build custom and lookalike audiences so we can reach similar people. This may involve sharing hashed or event-level data with Meta.

You can manage or opt out of interest-based advertising here:

  • Meta ad preferences: facebook.com/adpreferences
  • Digital Advertising Alliance: optout.aboutads.info
  • Network Advertising Initiative: optout.networkadvertising.org

5.How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only as described here:

  • Service providers / processors who operate the Services on our behalf, including our CRM and marketing platform (CourseCreator360 / GoHighLevel / LeadConnector), our sales CRM (Close), our data/backend provider (Supabase), our advertising and analytics partners (such as Meta), and video, email/SMS, and scheduling/meeting providers (such as Google Meet and Google Calendar).
  • Advertising partners, to measure and improve campaigns and build audiences, as described above.
  • Affiliates and referral partners, limited to confirming a referral was made.
  • Legal, safety, and compliance: to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect our rights, users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.
  • With your direction or consent.
Note for California residents: our use of advertising technologies like the Meta Pixel may be considered "sharing" (or a "sale") of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law, even though no money changes hands. See "US state privacy rights" to opt out.

6.Text messaging (SMS)

If you provide your mobile number and opt in, you agree to receive automated marketing and informational text messages from Breedwise about the case study, your call, and our programs. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

  • Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe;
  • Reply HELP for help, or email support@breedwise.io;
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

7.Email communications

We send transactional emails (such as your case study access and call confirmations) and marketing emails. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any such email; you may still receive transactional messages related to your bookings.

8.Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide the Services, run and measure our marketing, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When it is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

9.Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10.Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of marketing and certain advertising activities. To exercise a right, email support@breedwise.io. We will verify your request and respond as required by law. You may also unsubscribe from email (link in each email) and text (reply STOP) at any time.

11.US state privacy rights

If you are a resident of California or another US state with a comprehensive privacy law, you may have the right to: know the categories of personal information we collect and how we use and share it; request access to or deletion or correction of your personal information; and opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not knowingly sell personal information for money, and we do not sell the information of anyone we know to be under 16.

To exercise these rights, email support@breedwise.io with the subject "Privacy Request." To opt out of advertising-related sharing, you can also use the ad-preference and opt-out tools listed in Section 4 and your browser's Global Privacy Control signal, where honored. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

12.International users (GDPR / UK)

We are based in the United States and process information there. If you access the Services from outside the US, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in the US and other countries with different data-protection laws. Where the EU/UK GDPR applies, we process personal data under the legal bases described in Section 2 and honor applicable rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection). Contact support@breedwise.io.

13.Children's privacy

The Services are intended for adults 18 and older and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

14.Third-party links

Our Services may link to third-party websites and tools we do not control. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we are not responsible for them.

15.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Your continued use of the Services after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

16.Contact us

Questions or requests? Contact Breedwise Academy, LLC at support@breedwise.io.
