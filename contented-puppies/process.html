Ask Us Anything
Questions & Answers

Common questions

A few things families often ask. If your question is not here, we are glad to answer it personally.

How do I reserve a puppy?

Start by sending an inquiry. We will talk with you, answer your questions, and once we have found the right match, a deposit reserves your puppy. The deposit is applied toward your puppy's total.

Is a deposit required, and how much?

Yes, a deposit holds your chosen puppy and is applied to the total. Please contact us for current deposit details.

Do you offer pickup or delivery?

We are located in McClure, Pennsylvania, and welcome families to arrange a go-home visit. If you are traveling from a distance, reach out and we will discuss safe options together.

What health documentation comes with my puppy?

Your puppy's health records go home with them. Our dogs are in the process of genetic and OFA health testing, and we will share results and documentation as each is completed.

When can my puppy come home?

Puppies go home once they are fully weaned, well-socialized, and ready. We will confirm the go-home timing with you during the reservation.

What breeds do you raise?

We raise Yorkshire Terriers, Biewer Terriers, and Morkies, all family-raised in our home.

Do you keep a waitlist?

Yes. If we do not currently have the puppy you are hoping for, join our waitlist and you will be among the first to know when new litters arrive.

Have a question, or ready to begin?

We would love to hear from you and help you find your perfect companion.
