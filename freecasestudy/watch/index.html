Is This Call For You?

This call IS for you if…

  • You’re an ethical breeder in the U.S. with at least one litter a year.
  • You’re tired of pups sitting past 8 weeks or discounting to move them.
  • You want your program to reliably contribute to your household income.

This call is NOT for you if…

  • You’re just doing one “fun” litter and never breeding again.
