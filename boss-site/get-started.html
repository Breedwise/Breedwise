Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you apply

Do you build everything for me?+

B.O.S.S. combines three kinds of support: some assets are built for you (your content shoot, website, brand), some systems are built with you (your buyer process, sales systems, workflows), and some areas are guided by experts (genetics, whelping, neonatal). Your exact scope is documented before you enroll.

Do I need a big following or fancy equipment already?+

No. A large following isn't a prerequisite. You film simple clips we direct you through on your phone, and we handle the rest. We focus on a clearer brand, purposeful content, and systems that turn real interest into qualified buyer conversations.

Will you sell my puppies for me?+

No. You remain responsible for evaluating families, making placement decisions, and managing your customer relationships. We build and improve the systems and communication that support those responsibilities — we don't take them over.

Is this suitable for a brand-new breeder?+

B.O.S.S. is designed for established breeders with a functioning program and real operational needs. If you're just getting started, other BreedWise educational options are a better first step.

How much is it, and is there a guarantee?+

It's a premium, capped, hands-on partnership — a meaningful investment, with exact pricing and scope covered on your fit call and documented in writing before you enroll. It's backed by our guarantee: profitable in months, or your investment back, under the specific written terms of your agreement.

What do you actually need from me?+

Your participation. You make key decisions, provide materials, review important deliverables, and follow through on the implementation steps in your plan. The systems work because you run them — we build them to fit how you already work.

Do you guarantee financial results?+

Results depend on your starting point, participation, market, breeding outcomes, and other factors, so we don't promise specific income. Any contractual guarantee applies only under its written eligibility requirements and terms.
