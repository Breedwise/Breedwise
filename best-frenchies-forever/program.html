Temperament First

How we raise them

Temperament is everything in a family dog. Our puppies are raised inside our home and handled every day, so they grow up confident, calm, and ready to bond with you.

  • Raised underfoot in a busy family home, around everyday sights and sounds.
  • Handled every day from the first days so puppies are used to people and touch.
  • Early socialization for calm, friendly, well-adjusted temperaments.
  • Lifetime support from Russell for every family we place a puppy with.
