Before you apply

How is this different from a course?

A course hands you information and hopes you apply it. B.O.S.S. builds the systems with you and keeps you moving until they are running. You leave with a website, a CRM and operating systems, not a to-do list.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. We configure the core systems with you and our team keeps the pathway moving. You bring your program and your effort. We bring the build and the structure.

What exactly gets built?

Your baseline and 120-day pathway, your website, your CRM and lead system, and the workflows, dashboards, templates and trackers your pathway requires, across both the breeding and business sides of your program.

How much of my time does it take?

It is a 120-day commitment. There are formal reviews at Day 30, 60, 90 and 120, monthly strategy sessions, and a private channel so you can get unstuck quickly. The more you show up, the more gets built.

What happens after the 120 days?

The high-touch build phase ends on purpose, so it never becomes unlimited access. If you want ongoing support to keep operating, measuring and improving, B.O.S.S. Continuation is available at $297/mo.
