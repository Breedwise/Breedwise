Leaderboard standards

Rules & safeguards

Proof required

Implementation and wins only count when posted in Skool or shared on a call. No proof, no points.

Quality over volume

Staff can lower a Skool engagement score if points came from low-value activity, like one-word comments or repeat posts.

No double-dipping

One action earns points in one category only. A win shared on a call counts as a win, plus the participation points for speaking.

Tie-breaker

The member with more combined Implementation + Wins points ranks higher.

Monthly reset

Everyone starts at 0 on the 1st of each month.
