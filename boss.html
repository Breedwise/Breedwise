BOSS™
BreedWise Operating System & Standards
120-Day Personalized Implementation Program

Identify what your breeding program and business need most. Build the systems. Implement them with our team. Measure the progress.

BOSS is not primarily a course. It is a 120-day implementation pathway supported by training, tools, technology, experts, accountability, and direct access to the BreedWise team.

Assess→Prioritize→Build→ Implement→Support→Measure→Reassess
The Method

The BOSS™ Method

Ten moves. Complex infrastructure underneath, a radically simple experience on top.

01
01 — ASSESS

Know where you stand.

Every member begins with a structured baseline across their breeding program and business.
Included
  • BOSS Baseline Assessment
  • Business + breeding program review
  • Current systems inventory
  • KPI baseline
  • Priority / gap identification
  • Definition of the member's desired 120-day outcome
OutputA documented starting point.
02
02 — PRIORITIZE

Know what matters now.

BreedWise turns the assessment into a personalized 120-Day BOSS Implementation Pathway — not the same curriculum for everyone.
The pathway determines
What to build→What to learn→ What tools to use→What to implement→What to measure
OutputA clear 120-day implementation roadmap with priorities, milestones, owners and deadlines.
The pathway is an actual project plan — not merely access to tailored trainings.
03
03 — BUILD

Install the infrastructure.

BreedWise helps configure the core technology and workflows, not simply hand over software.
  • BreedWise Website — built around their program, positioning, credibility and buyer journey.
  • CRM + Lead Management System — their buyer pipeline and lead-management infrastructure.
  • System Configuration — core technology and workflows configured for implementation.
Scope is defined separately in the SOP: what's built by BreedWise / what's configured / what's templated / what the member owns / revisions / integrations / hosting / ongoing support — so "website built" never becomes unlimited agency work.
04
04 — IMPLEMENT

Learn it. Use it. Build it.

Not "watch the entire course." Instead: this is what you're building next — here's the training you need to build it.
BOSS Training Library
  • Simple, complete training organized around the systems they're actually implementing.
BOSS Tools & Templates
Frameworks→SOPs→Checklists→ Calculators→Trackers→Scripts→ Templates→Dashboards→Playbooks
PrincipleDon't just teach it. Give them the tool to do it — on both the breeding and business sides.
05
05 — SUPPORT

Your team, alongside theirs.

The member's BOSS Implementation Channel — a private 1:1 Slack channel for 120 days. Not "Slack access."
Questions, roadblocks, feedback, decisions and progress all flow through one place
Jennifer Implementation, accountability, project management
Austin Business strategy and decision-making
Yasmin Brand / social strategy within scope
Dr. Fontaine Veterinary / scientific education within appropriate educational boundaries
06
06 — COACH

Direct access when decisions matter.

Direct sessions to review priorities, diagnose constraints and determine next actions.
  • Monthly 1:1 with Austin — four private implementation / strategy sessions across the 120 days.
  • Quarterly Veterinary / Scientific Education Session — one direct session, kept educational and program-preparedness focused (not individualized diagnosis or treatment).
07
07 — MANAGE

Someone owns the implementation.

Jennifer — BOSS Project Manager / Member Success. The member never has to wonder "what am I supposed to do next?" — that's one of the things BOSS solves.
Jennifer owns the member's implementation rhythm
Pathway→Milestones→Accountability→ Resources→Roadblocks→Reviews→Next actions
08
08 — REVIEW

Progress doesn't stay subjective.

Formal reviews at every checkpoint, each ending the same way.
30-Day What started? What's complete? What's blocked?
60-Day What's working? What's behind? What needs adjustment?
90-Day What has been installed? What needs optimization?
120-Day What was built? What changed? What remains?
Every review endsNext priority → Next action → Owner → Deadline.
09
09 — MEASURE

See the progress.

BOSS Progress Dashboard — one place to see everything.
  • Implementation Progress — systems completed / underway / blocked
  • Business KPIs — demand, inquiries, qualified buyers, conversion, pricing, litter economics and other relevant metrics
  • Breeding / Program Preparedness — preparedness, recordkeeping, monitoring and system-completion indicators
  • Milestones — 30 / 60 / 90 / 120
Breeding KPIs measure preparedness and implementation — never promises of pregnancy rates, litter size, puppy survival or medical outcomes.
10
10 — REASSESS

Prove what changed. Decide what's next.

At Day 120, the baseline is run again — and the loop closes.
Where you started→What you identified→What you built→ What you implemented→What changed→What's still missing
ThenTheir Next BOSS Roadmap — remaining gaps and the member's next priorities, so the first 120 days never feel artificially unfinished.
