Academy Isn't for Every Breeder

Apply if…

  • You're already breeding, or actively preparing your first litter within 12 months
  • You have a real demand, pricing, profitability, placement, or breeding-program challenge
  • You're willing to implement changes
  • You're prepared to invest in your program if Academy is the right fit

Don't apply if…

  • Breeding is only a "someday" idea
  • You're looking for shortcuts
  • You're unwilling to change how you market, price, or operate

Not sure? Apply anyway. If Academy isn't the right starting point, we'll tell you.
