Weekly Block Schedule

The operating framework, Monday–Friday. Exact times can be adjusted internally — the structure and Definition of Done stay fixed.

Monday — Diagnose + Prioritize
Roadmap availability up to 3h

Member Health review: new · Academy · Accelerator · GREEN/YELLOW/RED · missed deliverables · attendance · portal engagement · failed payments · upcoming expirations · outstanding escalations.

AL + CM beginning‑of‑week meetings: last week's numbers · misses · priorities · deadlines · member concerns · top 3 responsibilities.

Tuesday — Member Execution
Roadmap availability up to 3h

Check‑ins in priority order: RED → YELLOW → new members → Accelerator behind → normal proactive touches.

Calls: host/coordinate community calls · masterclasses · Q&A · strategy sessions · Accelerator sessions. Log all meaningful interactions.

Wednesday — Delivery
Roadmap availability up to 3h

Accelerator / Intensive delivery: deliverables scheduled & shipped · assets uploaded · portal updated · replays & notes posted · questions routed · specialists aware.

Academy onboarding: host weekly onboarding as scheduled.

Thursday — Implementation + Coaching
Roadmap availability up to 3h

Expert / Accelerator coordination: vet sessions · strategy sessions · Q&A · experts · call reminders · asset organization.

AL + CM coaching: member communication · accountability · response quality · follow‑through · community rhythm · deadlines. If something repeatedly breaks, create or refine a protocol.

Friday — Close the Loop
Roadmap availability up to 3h if demand requires

AL + CM end‑of‑week review: promised vs completed vs missed · why · what changes next week.

Dashboard + proof: update Member Success data · collect wins, testimonials, screenshots, videos, case‑study candidates · prep so Monday doesn't begin in chaos.

Member Health System

GREEN

  • Engaged
  • Progressing
  • Implementing
  • Attending appropriately
  • Completing expected actions

No extraordinary intervention. Continue normal cadence.

YELLOW — early warning

Missed calls · missing assignments · declining portal activity · deliverable delays · slow responses · confusion · mild dissatisfaction · payment concerns.

Required action
  • Identify issue → contact member
  • Determine obstacle → recommit
  • Assign next action
  • Document → set follow‑up date

RED — critical

Cancellation request · major dissatisfaction · significant implementation failure · repeated disengagement · deliverables seriously behind · explicit intention to leave.

Required outreach within
24 HOURS

Escalate to Austin if no response · beyond Jennifer's authority · materially financial/legal · core promise disputed · cancellation still likely.

Member Check‑in Cadence

RED  Touch/attempt within 24 hrs of designation. Continue until resolved, escalated, or member explicitly disengages.

YELLOW  At least 1 proactive intervention weekly while YELLOW, until returned to GREEN or moved to RED.

GREEN  Normal program/community cadence. Don't create unnecessary 1:1 workload where members are healthy.

New members — proactive touches, first 30 days
  • Touch 1 — Roadmap
  • Touch 2 — Post‑onboarding / first‑action confirmation
  • Touch 3 — Week 2 progress check
  • Touch 4 — 30‑day health check

Additional touches based on risk/status.
