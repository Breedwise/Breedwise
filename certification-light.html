Founding Breeder
Certification

A verified, official certification for established members, issued while the full Certified Breeder™ program is built. It validates verified knowledge, a real veterinary relationship, a seen facility, documented standards and ethics. It does not certify or guarantee medical outcomes, pregnancy, litter size, puppy health, or profitability.
Launch target · Nov 28, 2026 For 12+ month members Verified, not paper-only Transitions to the full cert
Why a Founding certification. Dr. Fontaine's review of the full system: the structure is right (gates, evidence, audit, disclaimers), but a checklist that confirms paperwork was "submitted" is not the same as verifying a standard was met. His conclusion: a smaller certification that is actually verified is worth more than a full one audited on paper. This Founding certification keeps fewer requirements, and verifies the three that matter.

What it is

An official, provisional certification for members who have been with BreedWise for a year or more and have done the work. It is issued now, under a standard that is deliberately smaller than the full system but fully verified, and certificants transition into the full Certified Breeder™ program as it is completed. It is a real credential, not a participation badge, and it will keep expanding.

Who is eligible

  • Member in good standing for 12+ months
  • All required science modules completed (mandatory for every path, including legacy, no credit given)
  • Minimum curriculum completion reached
  • No unresolved ethics or conduct concerns
The three things BreedWise verifies — not just declares
01 · SCIENCE

Completed the science, and can prove it

Every science module is required and verified against the record — legacy included, no credit. The science section of the assessment must pass on its own, independent of the overall score. Weak science can never be carried by business questions.

Verified by BreedWise
02 · VETERINARY

A real relationship with their vet

A short attestation signed by the clinic: active client, breeding dogs seen in the last 12 months, program discussed with the vet, emergency plan reviewed. "Knows when to call the vet" becomes scenario questions in the assessment.

Signed by the clinic
03 · FACILITY

The facility has been seen

A recorded or live video walkthrough against a defined minimum list (whelping/neonatal area, hygiene, isolation, housing, climate, capacity vs. number of dogs). Reviewed against the list; anything that looks wrong is escalated to Dr. Fontaine.

Verified by video
Carrier & abnormal results. The hardest scientific question in the system. It stays at "disclosed, and the pairing plan is documented." BreedWise never states that it approves an individual dog for breeding.

How it is run (so the dates hold)

  • Angela owns Gate D genetics & health-testing standard end to end, including the carrier policy.
  • Audits are run by a trained reviewer against the rubric. Dr. Fontaine takes scientific escalations only — carrier results, unclear health documents, a facility that looks wrong on video.
  • If either condition does not hold, the launch date moves.
