Regina — Inbound Sales Setter → Closer Track

A 30-day pilot for a new seat. Regina is not taking over social media strategy. Her job is to turn the attention Yasmin and Austin create into conversations, TPB customers, qualified Academy appointments, and eventually closed revenue.

Set the money language straight first: do not call the Academy setter payout "5 to 10%." The examples ($150 on $1,500 collected, $300 on $6,000+) are a tiered bounty. Make it explicit so nobody argues later about what a percentage meant.

04

When Regina Becomes the Closer

a different comp plan
Start at 10% of net cash collected on Academy sales Regina personally closes. If someone pays $1,500 today toward a $6,000 obligation, she earns $150 now; when BreedWise later collects the remaining $4,500, she earns another $450 as that cash is collected, assuming the account stays eligible under the written plan. 10% of collected revenue is a defensible high-ticket closer structure. Dial A Closer

Do not stack

When Regina sets and closes her own deal, she does not earn the $300 setter commission plus the 10% closer commission. One or the other.

Later (not yet)

  • Company-set lead Regina closes: 10%
  • Regina self-sources, qualifies and closes: potentially 12.5%

Do not introduce 12.5% yet. Let her prove she can close first.

05

Her Path to $6,500 – $7,000 / Month

The biggest issue with the original idea: she probably cannot realistically replace a $6,500–$7,000/month job as only a setter under these economics. At the max $300 setter payout: $6,500 − ~$867 base = $5,633 in commission, or about 19 Academy closes a month at $300 each. For $7,000, about 21. That is not a setter's job; that is a closer's.

The real path: setting creates the opportunity, closing creates the career

At a $6,000 average upfront collection, $60K is about 10 closed Academy customers a month — and Regina alone would be producing ~60% of the cash for the $100K/month objective. Do not promise her $7K/month. Show her the math required to earn it.

Regina's Career Ladder

show her this literally
Stage 1Inbound DM Setter

Objective: master conversations and qualification.

Owns: comment responses, DM conversations, lead qualification, TPB routing, Academy routing, appointment booking, follow-up, CRM documentation, no-show recovery.

Earns: $200/week + $20 TPB conversions + Academy setter commissions.

Stage 2Senior Setter / Closer Apprentice

Keep setting. Add: shadow Austin's calls, study winning and losing calls, learn the Academy offer and discovery framework and objections, role-play, begin running discovery portions, run supervised calls, review every call after.

Setting and closing are different skills. The transition is about downstream meeting quality, discovery ability, shadowing, role-play and supervised real calls, not appointment volume. DrillCall

Stage 3Junior Closer

Regina takes selected qualified calls; Austin reviews them. Warmest, lower-risk opportunities go to Regina; complex situations go to Austin. Now track her own: held calls, qualified calls, close rate, cash collected, follow-up closes, refunds / cancellations.

Stage 4Full Closer

Regina owns a meaningful portion of the Academy calendar: convert qualified opportunities into appropriate Academy customers while protecting customer fit and company economics. 10% of collected revenue becomes the main income engine.

Her DM Playbook

do not freestyle

1 · Engage

Someone comments. Acknowledge publicly when appropriate. The comment-to-DM automation delivers the first response; Regina takes over once they actually engage in the DM. Build the human handoff around Meta's supported messaging flow. Meta / Instagram API

2 · Start a real conversation

Not "Want to book a call?" Instead: "Curious — are you currently breeding, or still preparing for your first litter?" Now she has context.

3 · Understand

How established are they? What are they struggling with? What have they tried? What do they want to change? Why now? Education or implementation?

4 · Route

  • TPB — need clarity, education, fundamentals, tools, or not yet right for high-ticket → send TPB VSL.
  • Academy — established breeder + meaningful problem + wants implementation + urgency + resources → book call.
  • Nurture — not ready for either → appropriate marketing path.

5 · Book

Book, confirm time, record qualification notes, source, primary pain, desired outcome, relevant context, so Austin walks onto the call informed.

6 · Follow up

No response is not a dead lead. Regina owns follow-up until the lead books, buys TPB, explicitly declines, is disqualified, or reaches the defined nurture point.

08

What Regina Should NOT Do

She is not becoming the Social Media Manager. Yasmin still owns content and distribution. Regina owns the sales conversations social media creates — not content calendars, filming, editing, analytics strategy, or what gets posted.
  • Give veterinary advice
  • Make scientific claims
  • Promise outcomes
  • Negotiate Academy pricing
  • Invent payment plans
  • Offer discounts
  • Change guarantees
  • Promise certification
  • Handle Member Success issues
  • Recommend Academy just because they can afford it
Until she is certified internally as a closer, she does not try to close Academy in the DMs. Her job is to create enough desire and clarity to get the right person onto the call.

The 30-Day Pilot

Week 1 · Learn + Execute

Access, offer training, qualification training, scripts, CRM training; begins handling conversations. Austin reviews daily. Optimize for correct routing, not volume.

Week 2 · Improve

Review which conversations became TPB, Academy, or died; where people objected; where Regina talked too much or asked too few questions. Update the playbook.

Week 3 · Closing training

Shadow Austin's Academy calls. For each: why did they buy or not, the real pain, the objection, how Austin responded, would Regina have qualified them. Start role-play.

Week 4 · Readiness review

Evaluate conversation quality, qualification accuracy, booked-call quality, show rate, downstream close rate, TPB conversions, revenue, follow-up and CRM discipline, coachability. Then decide — on evidence, not feel.

The decision at day 30: continue as Setter, or advance to Setter + Closer Apprentice.

The Opportunity to Present to Regina

Do not tell her "I'm giving you a $200/week Instagram job." Tell her this:

"I want to build you a path from Setter to Closer inside BreedWise.

We're going to start by giving you ownership of inbound conversations from Instagram. Your job is to turn those conversations into the right next step: TPB when that's the right fit, or a qualified Academy call when they need deeper implementation.

You'll have a weekly base plus commissions tied to the revenue you help generate. As you prove you can consistently qualify strong opportunities, I'm going to train you on the closing side as well.

The long-term opportunity isn't to make $200/week answering DMs. It's to become capable of generating $6,500 to $7,000+ per month by becoming a full-cycle sales professional who can qualify and eventually close BreedWise customers. The income isn't guaranteed; the math has to be supported by your performance and by the lead volume we generate. But I want you to see exactly what the path looks like."
$200/week isn't the opportunity. The opportunity is that BreedWise pays her to learn the front of the sales process, pays commissions while she learns, then develops her into a 10%-of-collected-cash closer. If she can personally close ~$60K/month, that's ~$6,867/month for her, while producing ~60% of the cash for the $100K/month objective. Her comp scales when the revenue scales.
