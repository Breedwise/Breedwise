A letter from our family

Why we do this

Stamyna started in 2021, when Allen and Madelyn were sophomores in college — one Boerboel, a small apartment, and a dog named Star who was our first baby. She came along on dates to the park and curled up with us for movie nights. Everywhere we took her, people stopped to ask what she was, where they could get one, and whether we'd ever breed. We realized we wanted to give other families that same access — done right.

So we decided to commit our lives to this — not only for our own family, but for the betterment of the breed. From our home in Milton, Georgia, we raise every dog to the true Boerboel standard, and we do it with faith at the center of everything.

What has never changed is the belief that a great Boerboel starts with a great foundation: sound parents, an attentive home, and a family that treats every dog as its own. That is the standard we hold ourselves to, litter after litter.

Allen & MadelynFounders
