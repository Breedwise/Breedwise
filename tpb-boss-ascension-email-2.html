What B.O.S.S. actually is

Hi {{contact.first_name}}, let me be specific, because “implementation” can mean anything.

B.O.S.S. is a bounded engagement where our team builds one priority system with you and gets it running. It follows a simple loop:

Diagnose. We find the one priority that will move your program most right now.
 
Build. Our team builds the actual system with you, not a worksheet about it.
 
Operate. We put it into use in your program so it runs every litter.
 
Measure and reassess. We check whether it worked, then decide what is next.

It is built with you, not handed to you. It does not replace The Profitable Breeder, and it is not a bespoke program that rebuilds your whole operation. It is a focused 120 day build on the priority that matters most.

If you want to see whether your priority is a fit, the next step is a short call. We look at where your program is, name the one system to build first, and tell you honestly if B.O.S.S. is the right move. If it is not, we will say so.
