How It Works

100 Points. Reset Every Month.

Every active member can earn up to 100 points each month. The leaderboard resets on the 1st. 60 of the 100 available points come directly from implementation and measurable progress. The goal isn't to reward whoever posts the most. It's to recognize members who are putting what they learn into practice.

Category 01

System Implementation — 40 Points

The heart of the leaderboard. Earn points by building systems, putting them into operation, and measuring how they're working.

BUILD → IMPLEMENT → MEASURE
BUILD
Create or document a system, process, tool, SOP, tracker, marketing plan, buyer process, pricing structure, etc.
+3 Points
IMPLEMENT
Put the system into active use inside your breeding program or business.
+4 Points
MEASURE
Review the system after using it and provide evidence, feedback, or a relevant KPI.
+3 Points
Fairness Rule:
Points are awarded for member implementation — not simply because BreedWise created an asset for you. Members do not automatically receive points because an asset was built or configured for them. Points are earned when you complete your responsibilities, adopt the system, put it into operation, and measure it.

Leaderboard Standards

Rules & Safeguards

Proof Required
Implementation and progress must be demonstrated in Skool, through an approved submission, or on an eligible call.
Implementation > Consumption
Watching a module alone earns zero leaderboard points. What you do with it matters.
No Done-With-You Advantage
Done-with-you assets do not automatically earn points. Member adoption and implementation do.
No Double-Dipping
The same action cannot be repeatedly submitted across categories.
Progress Is Relative
A newer or smaller breeder can outperform an established breeder by making more meaningful progress from their own baseline.
Quality Over Volume
BreedWise may reject low-value submissions, repetitive activity, or obvious point farming.
Tie-Breaker:
If two members finish with the same score, the member with the highest combined System Implementation + Measured Progress score ranks higher.
